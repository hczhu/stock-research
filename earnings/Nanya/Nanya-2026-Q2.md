- **Source**: Nanya Technology 2026 Q2 (CQ2 / Jun-26 quarter) earnings summary + SemiAnalysis/company revenue & gross-margin chart
- **Quarter covered**: Calendar 2026 Q2
- **Theme**: Legacy/commodity DRAM tightness play. Record revenue and peak margins driven almost entirely by **ASP**, not bits — a pure pricing upcycle. DDR5 is capacity-capped, not demand-capped.

- ## Headline
	- **Revenue \$2.61B**, +68% q/q, +684% y/y — **all-time high**; ~3x the prior-cycle peak (up from 1.8x just one quarter ago).
	- **Gross margin 79.5%**, +11.6pp q/q (from 67.9%), +30.5pp from 49% in 4Q25 — **peak margins**.
	- **ASP-driven**: ASP up >60% q/q (vs >70% in 1Q26) and >500% y/y; **bit shipments flat q/q**.
	- **1H26 revenue \$4.2B** ≈ 4x FY23 (\$958M) + FY24 (\$1.1B) combined.

- ## Product Mix & Demand
	- **DDR4/LPDDR4: 60-70% of revenue**; DDR5 ~10%; DDR3/LPDDR3 ~10%; rest LPDDR5/auto/industrial.
	- **DDR5 is capacity-capped, not demand-capped.**
	- **AI infra and servers now >20% of revenue.**

- ## Guidance & Contracts
	- CY26 bit-shipment target **held at +high-teens% y/y** (shipments flat q/q; allocating to LTAs given low inventory).
	- Q3 guided to further improvement, **operating margin >73.7%**; 4Q roughly in line with 3Q.
	- Contract structure **~50% LTA**.

- ## Capacity & Capex
	- New fab **on schedule**: Phase 1 to **30K wspm by 2028**, full **45K wspm at US\$16B** total.
	- **FY26 capex \$1.65B**, ~30% WFE.
	- **EUV starts 2028**, but node not yet locked.

- ## Competition
	- **Dismisses CXMT as not direct competition.**

- ## Read-Through
	- Textbook late-cycle commodity-DRAM print: **price, not volume**, doing all the work (bits flat, ASP >500% y/y). Margins already at/above prior-cycle peak with company holding bit growth flat and steering supply into LTAs — consistent with the industry-wide supply-discipline / scarcity regime (see [[$MU]] SCA model in [[MU-2026-Q2]] and [[DRAM-memory-ssd-index-thesis]]).
	- Key risk is the mirror image of the setup: a pure-ASP upcycle unwinds hard if legacy-DRAM pricing rolls over — watch the DDR4/LPDDR4 spot/contract spread and whether the >70%→>60% q/q ASP deceleration continues to fade.
