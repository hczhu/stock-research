- ## AI Infrastructure & TPU-as-a-Product (strategic)
	- **First-ever recognition of external TPU-system sales** — TPUs delivered into *customers'* data centers (e.g., the **Blackstone** project). Mechanics: agreements sit in backlog → Google **builds inventory ahead** (a drag on cash from ops) → revenue recognized on delivery. **Small in Q2; ramps through 2026; vast majority of revenue in 2027.** Framed as **TAM expansion**, not a margin call-out (margins not broken out).
	- **Allocation priority stack** (stated repeatedly): **(1) frontier/AGI model development first**, then **(2) core products (Search, YouTube)** and **(3) Cloud serving** (Vertex, Gemini Enterprise), with **external "infrastructure" TPU demand balanced last** via customer-DC placements.
	- **Third-party compute as a bridge**: a **SpaceX** third-party compute deal cited; Google will **expand third-party capacity in Q3** to serve very large cloud customers through the shortage — **modest near-term cloud-margin pressure** but "highly ROI-positive over the multi-year deal." (Note the mirror image: Google is *both* selling TPUs externally *and* renting third-party capacity to bridge its own shortage.)
	- Infra offerings: broadest accelerator range (**Google TPU + Nvidia Vera Rubin**); a network to link **~1M accelerators across sites into one supercomputer**; software stack with native **JAX, PyTorch, vLLM, SGLang** (GPU↔TPU portability); new **agent-optimized Axion CPU, +30% perf/\$ vs peers.**
