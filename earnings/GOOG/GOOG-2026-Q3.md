- **Source**: Alphabet Q3 2026 earnings call transcript, user-provided (Sundar Pichai, Philipp Schindler, Thomas Kurian making his first appearance on the call, Anat Ashkenazi). Prior quarter: [[GOOG-2026-Q2]]. The 10-Q disclosure on TPU repurchase rights is covered separately in [[2026-10-29-google-tpu-repurchase-guarantees]].
- **Thesis**: The quarter split Alphabet in two. **Cloud accelerated to +103%** on GCP and the first meaningful TPU-system sales, while **Search slowed from +17% to +12%**, even though query growth is the fastest in five years. Search revenue was flat sequentially at \$63.3B. Management attributes the gap to FX, vertical one-offs, a tough comparison, and the **lower monetization of AI Mode queries**, and it is now turning on ads in AI surfaces globally. Funding both businesses has driven **quarterly free cash flow negative (−\$9.1B)**.

- ## AI's Impact on Search: Volume, Query Types, Monetization
	- **Volume**: Overall query growth is "the highest it's been in the last five years," and that includes **commercial queries**. Philipp says the **majority of AI Mode queries are incremental**, meaning they add to traditional Search rather than replacing it. Visual searches exceed **50 billion a month**. Google has stopped describing AI Mode as a separate product: AI Overviews, AI Mode, and traditional Search are presented as "one seamless experience." Q2 reported AI Mode at more than 1B MAU, and Q3 gave no update to that figure.
	- **Query types**:
		- **AI Mode queries are on average 3× longer** than traditional searches.
		- **1 in 5 AI Mode searches is multimodal** (voice, images, or documents).
		- Many AI Mode queries are **upper-funnel**: research and consideration rather than purchase intent. That is new commercial intent Google previously did not capture, but it sits further from the transaction.
		- The next query type is **agentic**: booking, form-filling, scheduling, purchasing. The Gemini app has added a task tab where users already type agent-style requests. The full agentic experience ships to enterprise first, then to consumers "later this year" starting with the Gemini app, and then to Search. The bottleneck is serving cost: Google is distilling **Carbon** so it can run at billion-user scale on compute, latency, and quality.
	- **Monetization is lagging usage, and management said so.** The Q2→Q3 Search deceleration, by driver:
	- | Driver of the Q2→Q3 Search slowdown | Management's account | Persists into Q4? |
	  |-------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------|
	  | **FX** | The "single largest driver" | Yes; Q4 FX headwind is slightly larger at spot rates |
	  | **Lapping the Q3'25 acceleration** | Cost **~3 points** of growth | Yes, **another "couple of points"** in Q4 |
	  | **Retail vertical** | APAC advertisers hit by the **EU ending its de minimis exemption** (likely cross-border marketplace sellers) | Policy-driven; no end date given |
	  | **Media & entertainment** | Q2 was inflated by the FIFA World Cup, with more matches and ad budgets in June than July | No; a one-time comparison effect |
	  | **AI Mode mix shift** | "Strong growth in AI Mode queries had an impact because of the **lower monetization** we were seeing in AI Mode" | Depends on how quickly AI Mode ads scale |
	- The last row is the first time management has tied AI search to a revenue headwind. Usage is shifting to a surface that monetizes worse than the "ten blue links" it replaces. **Bears have argued for two years that this would show up in revenue, and it now has, though only as one of five drivers.**
	- **The fix being deployed**:
		- Ads in AI Overviews and AI Mode are scaling to more users and queries.
		- **AI Mode ads are going international for the first time**.
		- **Multi-turn ads in AI Mode** are launching in the U.S.
		- Supporting evidence: sponsored and organic product listings in AI Mode get **similar click-through rates**, and user research shows people are "comfortable with ads on these surfaces."
	- **Ad-quality signals**:
		- **Product listing ads** got the biggest update in a decade: user satisfaction rose **19%** and dissatisfaction fell by more than half.
		- **Discover Ads** personalization delivered **18% incremental advertiser value**.
		- Gemini generated **1.5B+ campaign assets** for advertisers in Q3, **more than 7× YoY**.
		- **Telstra** on AI Max saw conversions **+52%** and cost per order **−36%** from long-form and multilingual queries.
		- **AI Max** now supports A/B testing of budgets and ROI targets across multiple campaigns.
	- **Agentic commerce plumbing**:
		- **Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP)** now enables direct checkout for **hundreds of thousands of brands** (Chewy, Nike, Tapestry). In Q2 the named partners were Target and Steve Madden.
		- It is paired with the **Agent Payments Protocol** and **Universal Cart**. Universal Cart is designed so retailers keep the customer relationship.
		- Management's monetization argument for agents is that "there's almost never only one perfect option." Its example is an agent presenting an insurance product alongside a sponsored alternative with a different premium and deductible. **Ads move from the results page into the agent's shortlist.** Sundar frames agents as expansion, the same way AI chat expanded query volume.
	- **Net read**: The usage side of the Search debate is resolved in Google's favor: volume is up, queries are longer and more commercial, and the activity is incremental. The open question is now purely **monetization rate on AI surfaces**. Q4 carries more FX, a couple more points of lapping, and an AI Mode ad rollout that is only beginning. The test is whether Search growth re-accelerates in 1H27 as international and multi-turn AI Mode ads mature.

- ## Search, YouTube, and Network: Growth Quarter over Quarter
	- | Line | Q2 2026 YoY | Q3 2026 YoY | Q3 revenue |
	  |------------------------------------|------------------|------------------|---------------------------------------|
	  | Google Search & other | +17% | **+12%** | \$63.3B, **flat sequentially** |
	  | YouTube ads | +13% | **+9%** | \$11.2B; brand led, living room >⅓ of YouTube ads |
	  | Network | −1% | +1% | \$7.5B |
	  | Subscriptions, platforms, devices | +15% | +11% | \$14.3B; YouTube Music/Premium and Google One AI plans |
	  | Google Services total | +15% | **+11%** | \$96.3B; op margin 40.7% (from 41.8%) |
	- **Every ad line slowed except Network.** FX is common to all of them, so the comparison against Cloud is cleaner than it looks: Services is now growing at roughly a ninth of Cloud's rate.
	- YouTube: **150M+ Music/Premium subscribers** including trials. Subscriptions are growing faster than ads. **Amazon joined YouTube Shopping**, which lets U.S. creators tag Amazon products and turns videos into a direct path to purchase. **1.3M+ channels** are enrolled in YouTube Shopping.

- ## Google Cloud: +103%, and TPU Systems Become a Business
	- | Cloud metric | Q2 2026 | Q3 2026 |
	  |-------------------------------|-------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------|
	  | Revenue growth | +82% (\$24.8B) | **+103% (\$30.7B)**, +\$5.9B sequentially |
	  | Operating margin | 35.6% | **36.2%** (from 23.7% a year ago) |
	  | Backlog | \$514B | **\$561B** (+\$47B) |
	  | Share recognized in 24 months | >50% | **"just over 55%"**, implying roughly \$310B |
	  | First-party API tokens/min | ~22B | **~32B**, +40% QoQ and >4× YoY |
	- GCP was "the vast majority" of revenue, and Cloud growth **excluding TPU systems was "again incredibly strong."** No ex-TPU growth rate was given, although an analyst asked for it directly. TPU-system hardware cost is called out as a driver of the **+32% in other cost of revenue**.
	- **Margin outlook turns down near term**: third-party "bridging" compute came online only at the very end of Q3 and **ramps in Q4**, with management guiding to "margin headwinds in the near term." The 36.2% margin is likely a local peak.
	- **TPU customers**:
		- **~100 customers** use TPUs today, and "over 100 significant TPU customers" start in Google's cloud before moving on.
		- Named customers: **Anthropic** (both directly and **via Broadcom**), **Apple** (scaling foundation-model pre-training on the latest TPUs), Physical Intelligence, Citadel Securities, Jump Trading, Caltech, Cassava, and Cirrascale. A frontier lab transcribed as "Mirendil" was also named; the name is uncertain.
	- **Distribution widens past Google's own data centers**: TPU systems now go into customer data centers, into **Oracle** through a new partnership, and into **Crux AI**, a Blackstone joint venture. Management says "more in the works." Kurian adds that placement in third-party and customer facilities **avoids Google pre-building facilities**, which explains why the business can grow faster than Google's own data-center capacity.
	- **Software lock-in falls**: **TorchTPU**, built with **Meta**, runs PyTorch training and serving on TPUs. Kurian says it "opens up a much larger market." **TPU 8t** offers up to **2.7× training performance** and **TPU 8i** **80% better inference price-performance** versus the prior generation.
	- **Cash cycle**: a contract enters backlog, Google builds inventory (cash out), then delivers and bills (cash in). Kurian says **all TPU inventory is tied to signed contracts**. Revenue from these sales was recognized "to date" with **"more of it to come in through 2027."** Margins are not disclosed. Ross Sandler asked what revenue a **~\$35B-per-GW** TPU system generates and got no number.
	- **Platform logic, in Sundar's words**: TPU systems are a *platform*, like Chrome, Android, and Cloud, so selling them to competitors (Anthropic, Apple, Meta via TorchTPU) is deliberate: "you're putting it against market forces." He adds that "in the semiconductor industry, economies of scale matter": external volume lowers the unit cost of Google's internal TPUs. This is the strongest public statement yet of the external-TPU strategy discussed in [[2026-10-26-expert-call-nvidia-hyperscaler-concentration-external-tpu-amd-v10]].
	- **Gemini Enterprise depth**:
		- Over the past 12 months, **2,600+ customers** each used ≥100B tokens (versus >2,000 in Q2), and **~75 customers** each used ≥10T tokens.
		- **100M+ knowledge-work seats** sold across Google's AI services.
		- Customers using Gemini Enterprise in Q3'25 **more than quadrupled** their spend year over year.
		- 18 product lines exceed **\$1B** in annual revenue.
		- Security: **95%+ of Wiz customers** use its AI features.
		- BigQuery: query speed **+40%** and query processing cost **−50%** YoY. Agentic analytics is a direct driver of database consumption.

- ## Models: Gemini 4 Closes the Coding Gap Management Conceded in Q2
	- **Argon** (launched about two weeks before the call): developer-platform usage ramped **3.5× faster than Gemini 3** in week one, **despite capacity constraints**. Strongest in finance, legal, cybersecurity, and science.
	- **Carbon** (announced on the call): **#1 on the updated Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at 59** (see [[2026-10-29-artificial-analysis-v5-benchmarker-dilemma]] on how v5 reweights the index). It is going first to vetted cyber defenders and is being tested by the U.S. government, the same staged release pattern now standard for frontier cyber-capable models.
	- **Internal productivity**: Chrome, Ads, and Search teams using Argon shipped **35% more code with 30% fewer errors**. Sundar puts the inflection at Gemini 3.8 Flash and says Carbon is "a meaningful step up" from Argon. In Q2, coding was the area management conceded it was behind; on this call it became the headline.
	- **The RSI claim**: "I would expect the next 12 months to see continued acceleration because of RSI." This is a public CEO statement that **recursive self-improvement** is compressing Google's model cadence, and it fits with the "golden year of productivity" framing for 2027.
	- **The Gemini app reached 1B users** (950M MAU in Q2). Other launches: **Genie 4**, a real-time world model for training robots and agents.

- ## Capital, Cash Flow, and Commitments
	- | Item | Q2 2026 | Q3 2026 | Read |
	  |------------------------------------------|----------------------|----------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------|
	  | CapEx (quarter) | \$44.9B | **\$51.4B** | FY26 guide is unchanged at \$195–205B |
	  | Servers & networking share of tech infra | ~60% | **~65%** | Mix is shifting toward short-life assets (6-year lives) |
	  | Free cash flow (quarter) | +\$5.9B | **−\$9.1B** | Includes TPU inventory build and a **\$5.2B EC fine** (2018 case) |
	  | Free cash flow, TTM | \$53.3B | **\$19.7B** | Fell by ~\$34B in one quarter |
	  | Operating cash flow, TTM | \$185.7B | **\$179.6B** | Declined despite +25% revenue: working capital is absorbing the TPU business |
	  | Long-term debt | \$98.2B | **\$125.1B** | +\$27B in a quarter |
	- **Contractual commitments**: **\$924B in future purchase commitments** plus **\$152B in pre-commencement leases and other obligations**. These are maximums and some include volume flexibility, but together they exceed **\$1 trillion**, roughly 2× annual revenue. The 2027 capex will rise "significantly," with details on the Q4 call.
	- **Margin structure**: consolidated operating margin fell to **31.8%** from 34% in Q2. **R&D +36%** on AI talent and depreciation. The Alphabet-level loss (shared AI R&D) widened to **\$7.7B** from \$5.8B.
	- **Mark-to-market swing**: OI&E was **−\$8.2B**, mostly an unrealized loss on the **SpaceX** stake after its stock fell in Q3, versus +\$9.8B in Q2. Net income and EPS declined YoY on this alone. Reported EPS now swings materially with a single equity holding.
	- **The ROIC defense**: Ashkenazi made a prepared statement that ROIC will stay "meaningfully above our cost of capital." She cited Search query growth, "tens of billions of dollars" of Cloud AI revenue year to date, and Google One AI plans. Management explicitly expects free cash flow to stay under pressure from capex and TPU inventory. **An unprompted ROIC statement in the same quarter free cash flow turned negative shows management knows this is the question the market will ask.**
