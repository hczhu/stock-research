- **Source**: Micron 2026 Q1 earnings call (prepared remarks)
- **Quarter covered**: Nov. 28, 2025 – Feb. 26, 2026 (Micron 2026Q1; reported Mar. 18, 2026)
- **Theme**: AI-driven, supply-constrained memory up-cycle — Micron is locking in multi-year demand via new Strategic Customer Agreements, ramping leading-edge 1γ DRAM / G9 NAND, and scaling HBM4 while stepping up capex for FY2027

---

- ## Headline
	- **Demand outstrips supply across the board**: both AI and traditional server demand are constrained by inadequate DRAM and NAND supply.
	- **AI tips the demand mix**: data-center DRAM + NAND bit TAM is set to **exceed 50% of total industry TAM for the first time in CY2026**.
	- **Pricing power is the story**: ASPs up ~**mid-60s% Q/Q** on only mid-single-digit Q/Q bit-shipment growth — i.e. the up-cycle is overwhelmingly price-driven.
	- Micron is converting the cycle into durable visibility (multi-year SCAs incl. its **first five-year deal**) and committing to a meaningful capex step-up in FY2027 to capture long-term AI demand.

---

- ## Strategic Customer Agreements (SCAs)
	- A new contract structure, **distinct from prior LTAs**: SCAs carry **specific commitments over a multi-year horizon**, improving visibility and stability in the business model.
	- Give customers more certainty to plan their own businesses while deepening long-term engagement across Micron's full product portfolio.
	- **Signed its first five-year SCA** this quarter — a notable lengthening of contracted demand visibility.

---

- ## Technology Roadmap — DRAM (1γ / 1δ)
	- **1γ (1-gamma)** is ramping ahead of every prior node:
		- fastest ramp to mature yields in company history; ramping volume faster than any previous node
		- on track to become a **majority of DRAM bit mix by mid-CY2026**
		- expected to become the **highest-volume node in Micron's history**
	- **1δ (1-delta)**: plans to **increase EUV adoption** using latest-generation EUV tools, which improve both cleanroom space efficiency and patterning as Micron scales to 1δ and beyond.

---

- ## Technology Roadmap — NAND (G9 / QLC)
	- **G9** node on track to be a **majority of NAND bits by mid-CY2026**; achieved a **record QLC (quad-level cell) bit mix** in the quarter.
	- **Co-locating R&D and high-volume manufacturing** at Boise and Singapore to speed time-to-market for leading-edge products.
	- Plans to **meaningfully increase R&D investment in FY2027** to address an "unprecedented" set of AI-era memory/storage opportunities.
	- **Quality leadership**: a clear majority of customers now rank Micron **No. 1 in quality**.

---

- ## Data-Center HBM (HBM4 / HBM4E)
	- **HBM4 36GB 12-high** entered **volume shipments in Q1 CY2026** (announced at NVIDIA GTC), designed for **NVIDIA Vera Rubin**; expected to reach **mature yields faster than HBM3E**.
	- **HBM4 16-high sampled**: **48GB per HBM cube**, a **+33% capacity** step over HBM4 12H.
	- **HBM4E** in development, **volume ramp in CY2027**, built on the production-proven **1γ** node — pitched as another step-function performance gain enabling a new generation of AI compute platforms.
	- **HBM4E customization** opens further differentiation and deeper, stickier R&D co-engagement with customers.

---

- ## Data-Center LP DRAM (low-power)
	- Micron pioneered **LP DRAM for the data center** — ~**1/3 the power** of DDR server modules.
	- Sampled the **industry-first 256GB LP SOCAMM2** (on 1γ), enabling **2TB capacity per CPU** — **4× the content vs a year ago**.
	- Rationale: rapid AI-inference growth is spawning **new architectures optimized for per-workload token economics**, where Micron's broad HBM + LP + DDR + SSD portfolio is a critical enabler.
	- Cited at GTC: **NVIDIA Groq 3 LPX** implements up to **12TB of DDR5** in a rack-scale architecture.
