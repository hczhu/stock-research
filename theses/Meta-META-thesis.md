- ## Main Narrative
	- **Building a better technology company** (Meta's own framing): "Everyday Meta builds new ways for people to feel closer. This is a fundamental human need that may be more important in today's complex world than ever. One day we hope to enable every person to feel as strong a sense of connection as you feel when you're physically with someone you love."
		- "We do leading work across a wide range of advanced technologies and then distill that into inspiring products that improve people's lives. We do this with AI to help you creatively express yourself and discover new content, with the metaverse to deliver a realistic sense of presence, with new media formats to create richer experiences, with encryption to let you communicate privately in more and more ways, and with business tools to help reach customers, create opportunity and grow the economy."
		- "Simply put: if you want to invent the future or apply the best ideas to reach people at the greatest scale, then Meta is the best place to do that."
	- **The holy grail**: "The holy grail of social experiences is the ability to feel like you're present with another person." – Mark Zuckerberg ([2016 F8 keynote, 53'](https://developers.facebook.com/videos/f8-2016/keynote/))
	- **Personal superintelligence (2026)**: Zuckerberg's stated goal is personal agents and business agents for billions of people, not just a chatbot. Meta builds frontier models in house because "AI agents get better when you fully optimize the stack." Muse Spark, the first model from Meta Superintelligence Labs, powers the upgraded Meta AI. Meta's AI philosophy is that AI should amplify individuals, not replace people (2026Q1 call).
	- **Personal AI assistant (Muse) upside (Nov 2026)**: Muse is the option on top of the ads business. It could add a new revenue stream (transaction fees and subscriptions) and raise the value of users Meta already monetizes.
		- **Vision**: a proactive agent that understands a person's goals and acts on them across shopping, productivity, creativity and everyday life. Zuckerberg: "We think that billions of Muses in a network at scale will be able to get more done for people and businesses by working together." Meta is building capacity to serve more than 1B people over the next few years, contingent on serving efficiency ([[META-2026-Q3]]).
		- **Adoption so far**:
			- | Signal | Figure | Source |
			  |---|---|---|
			  | Setups | More than 10M in the US and Canada in about six weeks | Meta, Q3 2026 call ([[META-2026-Q3]]) |
			  | Daily use | Roughly 25% of people who set it up use it on a given day | Meta, Q3 2026 call |
			  | App DAU | **10.24M** worldwide on Nov 13, 2026 (iPhone + Android), up from ~0.7M on Sep 20 and accelerating since late October | Similarweb estimate ([[2026-11-17-similarweb-meta-muse-10m-dau]]) |
			  | Launch curve | Like-for-like US iOS DAU 1.55× ChatGPT's at the same point after launch | Apptopia estimate ([[2026-09-21-apptopia-muse-launch-metrics-vs-chatgpt]]) |
			  | Shopping | Over 25% of weekly actives used shopping; average purchase above \$200, including travel | Meta, Q3 2026 call |
			  | Connectors | Nearly 30% of users connected at least one service | Meta, Q3 2026 call |
		- **Monetization paths** (management's framing, [[META-2026-Q3]]):
			- **Transaction fees**: a free core experience funded over time by merchant-paid fees on transactions Meta helps facilitate. Meta previewed the Personal Agent Protocol with Sierra, Walmart, Stripe and Shopify so a personal Muse can transact with merchants' agents. Li said transaction fees need more users and partners.
			- **Subscriptions**: the only direct Muse revenue at the Q3 call. Muse Wonder is available to paid users; more advanced models, extra tokens, and business or workplace features are planned paid tiers.
			- **Ads**: Li kept direct advertising open as a possibility.
			- **Higher value per user**: more than 20% of US Muse users already generate at least \$1,000 a year for the Family of Apps. Zuckerberg expects Muse could eventually exceed existing Family of Apps revenue per user in this group, a long-term view.
				- **Rough baseline**: existing US Muse users generate on average **several hundred dollars a year** of Family of Apps revenue. Meta management said "the average revenue per American user for our existing Family of Apps users is several hundreds of dollars annually," then clarified that "the average revenue that I was talking about before is the Family of Apps revenue for our existing Muse users." (Remarks supplied by the user; speaker and date not given.)
				- The more than 20% of US Muse users who generate at least \$1,000 a year sit above that Muse-user average.
		- **Structural edges**:
			- **A very generous free tier, funded by ads**: Meta can give Muse away because ads already pay for its data centers ([[2026-10-13-businessweek-meta-muse-openai-dots-agent-business-models]]).
				- | Offer | Muse | OpenAI Dots |
				  |---|---|---|
				  | Free use | Core service free. At launch, **up to 100M tokens a week** plus a virtual computer per user ([[2026-09-08-zuckerberg-heath-muse-personal-agent-strategy]]) | None |
				  | Paid tiers | Optional \$20 a month, or \$100 for higher usage limits | Only on plans costing at least \$100 a month |
				- The 100M figure is a weekly ceiling, not average use.
				- **The cost side**: management says "Muse is very compute-intensive to run. But we expect to improve its cost efficiency by more than 10x in the next year." The Q3 call named the levers: smaller and task-specific models, inference and product optimization, and moving suitable work to off-peak capacity. Even so, total serving costs are expected to rise ([[META-2026-Q3]]).
			- **Bundling**: one free, context-rich agent can absorb many low-frequency consumer jobs and become the default interface to the internet, removing the need for specialized apps and thin agent wrappers ([[2026-09-15-meta-muse-consumer-agent-app-disintermediation]]).
			- **Product craft and trust**: familiar distribution, progressive permissions, persistent memory and polished artifacts make the agent easier to trust and reuse ([[2026-09-13-how-i-ai-meta-muse-firsthand-product-review]]). Each user's Muse runs in an isolated cloud VM, with a separate Sentinel agent checking actions ([[2026-09-08-meta-muse-secure-vm-architecture]]).
			- **Glasses**: Zuckerberg said on the Q3 call that Muse would start coming to Meta Glasses in November 2026. Bosworth sees wearables as the ultimate surface for personal AI because they give it real-world context (see Anecdotes, 2026-02).
		- **What has to go right**:
			- **Serving cost**: Meta targets more than 10× better Muse cost efficiency in the next year, but still expects total serving costs to rise as usage grows (see the free-tier bullet above).
			- **Execution reliability**: browser-based shopping produced errors in a hands-on review, even when calendar, content and planning workflows worked ([[2026-09-13-how-i-ai-meta-muse-firsthand-product-review]]).
			- **Trust for buying**: consumer surveys show trust falls sharply as agents move from searching to buying, so transaction fees are the hardest revenue to earn ([[2026-10-13-businessweek-meta-muse-openai-dots-agent-business-models]]).
			- **Model quality**: see the Avocado anecdotes and the "Not on the cutting edge of LLMs" risk below.

- ## Secular Trends as Tailwinds
	- **Social network/media penetration in developing countries**: [other non-saturated and developing countries will follow the same adoption curve as the US](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/fact-sheet/social-media/). Younger generations will push the penetration rate higher over time.
	- **Advertising is shifting to the internet**: [internet ads keep growing](https://quip.com/7czQASLY7WVo/MetaFacebook-FB#temp:C:JEB03d1abec252220fc5d17f8784) faster than the whole market.
		- There is a massive secular shift away from third-party advertising marketplaces to first-party integrated advertising platforms, driven by limits on third-party cookies, regulation (particularly in Europe), and Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT).
		- The global advertising business is worth about **\$600B a year**, and at least half of that, and growing, is digital. A huge chunk is being overturned by regulation (GDPR, CCPA) on one side and platform companies (Chrome, iOS) on the other. Cookie-based, cross-site, third-party tracking is going to disappear, as is some of the tracking built into iPhones (IDFA). You can advertise based on what's on the page ("context") and on what someone did on your site ("first party"), but advertising based on what someone did on another site is going to look totally different.
		- The user-privacy shift is hurting internet ads' effectiveness.
	- **AI boosts interactions with people and virtual worlds**: LLMs and vision models.

- ## Durable and Unfair Competitive Advantages
	- (Translated from the doc's Chinese.)
	- 1. **The world's most powerful network of person-to-person connections.** These networks get more useful the more people use them. The core competitive advantage is scale, not technology, so new technology can't easily disrupt it. As living standards rise and smartphones spread, the user base still has a lot of room to grow; in theory, every market outside mainland China is Meta's market.
	  2. **Monetization is limited to advertising**, so there is a lot of room to expand.
	  3. **The founder is very young, with an extremely accurate and sharp strategic eye.** The Instagram and WhatsApp acquisitions show it; they closely resemble Tencent's creation of WeChat.
	  4. The visionary founder CEO.

- ## Anecdotes & Opinions
	- **2026-11, Hacker News commenters on Instagram ads** (front-page thread full of complaints about internet ads; shared by the owner on X, Nov 6, 2026; the thread link is truncated in the screenshot): bullish (Instagram ad targeting)

		- Meta ad revenue correlates more with online economic activity, while Google Search depends more on offline activity.
		- Google Search is more resilient in a downturn. Meta's outperformance at the COVID peak is better explained by the second reason. Alternatively, Google Search may not be as resilient as 2022 suggests, and its growth may just lag Meta's by one or two quarters.

Every \$1 spent on a website campaign through Advantage+ generated \$7 in returns, nearly as high as before ATT. One agency's clients earned 20–30% more revenue per ad dollar on Advantage+ than on other Meta campaigns.

	- **2023/02, a brand operator**: bullish. "Advantage+ is probably the biggest economic invention in terms of immediate impact in the last 18 months since iOS14 happened."

		- This is the third time Meta has tried to make social shoppable and then pulled back, which suggests a fundamental problem with social checkout.
		- About half of online shoppers think social is "a great place to discover new products," but three quarters prefer to check out on the brand site, because of trust and authenticity concerns, inventory synchronization, assortment, pricing and promotions, and missing faceted search. Only 17% of shoppers who bought via a social network and had to return the product would buy via social again.
		- Most brands don't want customers to check out on social: they need the customer data and consent, because profits come from repeat customers. A quarter of the brands Meta touted at the Instagram checkout launch no longer use it. "The pattern that clearly works is to drive traffic to the brand site to convert."

- ## Appendix — Data Points & Facts
	- **The reach of the Family of Apps (2023/01)**: the ITU estimates nearly 3B of the world's 8B people lack internet access. Excluding China's 400M+ connected users, where US social media is largely banned, Meta's apps are used monthly by 90% of the world's connected population and daily by 70%.
	- **Piper Sandler teen survey (10/2024)**, top social media platforms: TikTok 39%, Instagram 32%.
	- **Threat from TikTok and Snapchat (2023/10)**: TikTok improved slightly as US teens' favorite social platform (38%, +80 bps vs. spring 2023). Snap was #2 at 28%, followed by Instagram at 23%.
	- **Social media fatigue (2024/05)**: in [an October report](https://pro.morningconsult.com/analysis/social-media-posting-frequency-preferences-survey) from Morning Consult, 61% of US adults with a social-media account said they have become more selective about what they post.
	- **Generative AI tools for advertisers (2024/05)**: Meta launched generative AI tools for creating ad materials. They matter most for Meta's long tail of hundreds of thousands of small advertisers, many of them one person without a creative team; they level the playing field.
	- **Canada's news ban didn't reduce politics-oriented Facebook engagement** (preliminary research findings):
		- Six months after the ban, about 33% of Canadians still say they use Facebook or Instagram for Canadian political and current-affairs information.
		- Facebook Pages of national news outlets lost about 64% of their engagement, and local outlets about 85%. Almost half of local outlets stopped posting on Facebook within four months.

- ## Appendix — Terminology
	- **Customer data platform (CDP)**: marketing software that collects and unifies first-party customer data from multiple sources into a single view of each customer.
	- **ARPU**: total revenue in a geography in a quarter divided by the average of MAUs at the start and end of the quarter. MAUs here count only Facebook and Messenger users, though revenue from them is "the substantial majority" of the total.
	- **Lead ads**: tapping the ad opens a form pre-populated with the person's Facebook contact information, generating a qualified lead in a few taps.
