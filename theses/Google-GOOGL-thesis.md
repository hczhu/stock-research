- ## Appendix — Earnings Notes by Quarter
	- Converted from the doc's "Earnings & Filings & Proxies & Events" section. See [[GOOG-2026-Q2]] and [[GOOG-2026-Q3]] for the full notes.
	- ### 2026Q3
		- **AI Mode query monetization**: many upper-funnel queries carry new high-value commercial intent, and the majority are incremental. CTRs are similar between organic and sponsored product listings (shopping ads only). Google is scaling high-quality ads to more users and queries.
		- **Agentic experiences for businesses and consumers**: Google will build ad models for agentic use cases and go beyond them with subscriptions and other payment methods.
		- **Users**: YoY query growth is the highest in five years. 32B tokens per minute, +40% QoQ and 4× YoY.
		- **TPU**: meaningful TPU system revenue ramp in 2027. A TPU system combines hardware, system software, compilers and optical networking, assembled as a system. TPU systems will be available in customers' data centers. 100+ TPU customers.
	- ### 2026Q1
		- Strongest quarter ever for consumer AI plans, driven by Gemini App adoption. Paid subscriptions reached **350M**, with YouTube and Google One the key drivers.
		- Waymo surpassed **500K riders per week**. Search queries are at an all-time high. About 20% of search queries trigger ads, with upside.
		- Google will begin delivering TPU hardware to a select group of customers in their own data centers, with the vast majority of revenue in 2027.
	- ### Google Cloud Next 2026
		- **New TPU markets**: trading firms (e.g. Citadel) found that model inference performs better than traditional numerical computing for algorithmic trading. HPC demand from energy modeling, computational fluid dynamics and solid-state work.
		- First-party models process **more than 23T tokens per day via direct API**, up from 14T the prior quarter. For comparison, OpenAI generated 10T tokens per day in 2025.
	- ### 2025Q4
		- **2026 capex guidance raised to \$185B from \$175B**, funding Gemini for consumer services (Search AI Mode, YouTube AI creation, YouTube Ask), AI-improved ad quality and advertiser tools (including ads in AI Mode), and GCP AI demand.
		- **Consumers**: 325M paid subscriptions across consumer services. Gemini App at **750M MAU** vs. ChatGPT at 800M. People search more with AI, with longer, more conversational queries. 1 in 6 AI Mode queries is non-text (voice or images).
		- **Enterprise**: 8M paid Gemini Enterprise seats. Antigravity reached 1.5M WAU two months after launch. 10B tokens per minute through first-party model APIs (6B for OpenAI). In December alone, nearly 350 customers each processed more than 100B tokens.

	- ### YouTube on the TV screen
		- **Share of US TV viewing time by distributor (Nielsen, Oct 2024–2025)**: YouTube rose from ≈10.6% to ≈12.5% and is #1. Disney ≈11.7% → ≈10.7%; NBCUniversal and Paramount ≈8%; Netflix ≈7.5%.
		- **YouTube global viewing by device (Tubular Labs)**: TV rose from ≈34% in early 2023 to ≈44% in 2025, overtaking mobile (≈47% → ≈40%) in 2023. Desktop ≈8%, tablet ≈6%, game console ≈2%.
		- **US daily social media hours (Emarketer, Epyllion analysis)**: total average daily hours of use across Facebook, Instagram, YouTube and TikTok grew from 295M (2019) to 359M (2020), 398M (2021), 440M (2022), 472M (2023), 499M (2024) and 500M (2025). That is 122M more hours per day than in 2020/21, about 21 minutes for each of 341M people. YouTube is the largest block at ≈220M hours per day in 2025, and TikTok's growth has not come at its direct competitors' expense.
		- **US platform reach (Pew, survey of 5,022 adults, Feb–Jun 2025)**: share of US adults who ever use YouTube 84%, Facebook 71%, Instagram 50%, TikTok 37%, WhatsApp 32%, Reddit 26%, Snapchat 25%, X 21%, Threads 8%, Bluesky 4%, Truth Social 3%.
	- ### Gemini vs. other chatbots
		- **Cumulative worldwide downloads of generative AI apps since Jan 2023 (Sensor Tower, Dec 2025)**: ChatGPT ≈1.55B, Gemini ≈0.5B, then Doubao, DeepSeek, Adobe Acrobat Reader, Perplexity, Nova, Microsoft Copilot, Grok and ChatOn, each ≈0.2B or less.
		- **Average minutes per visit, desktop and mobile web (FT, to Oct 2025)**: Gemini climbed from ≈4.7 in early 2025 to ≈7.5 in late 2025, overtaking ChatGPT (≈6.5) and Claude (≈6.0).
		- **Generative AI web traffic share (Similarweb, 12 months to the chart date)**: OpenAI fell from ≈87% to ≈72%, while Gemini rose from ≈5% to ≈13%, the largest gain.
	- ### TPU, Anthropic and AI compute
		- **Anthropic and TPUs**: in 2024/11 Anthropic named AWS its "primary cloud and training partner" and said it works with AWS's Annapurna Labs on future Trainium chips. In 2025/10 it announced access to up to 1M Google Cloud TPU chips for training and serving Claude. In 2026-04 it agreed to access about **3.5GW of TPU-based compute starting in 2027**, as part of a commitment for multiple gigawatts. Anthropic had reached a \$30B revenue run rate.
		- **What 3.5GW is worth**: a 1GW AI data center costs \$35–80B, so 3.5GW is about \$120B. Cost breakdown per GW:
			- | Component | Share of cost | Notes |
			  |---|---|---|
			  | Compute and IT equipment | ≈40–50% | 1GW runs about 1M modern GPUs (GB200 or Rubin); hardware alone costs \$15–30B+ |
			  | Networking | ≈10–13% | Switches, optical interconnects, InfiniBand or Ethernet fabrics; \$3.5–5B |
			  | Power infrastructure | ≈10% | Transformers, UPS, backup generators |
			  | Construction and real estate | ≈10% | |
			  | Cooling | ≈4–5% | Liquid cooling for 1,000W+ chips; ≈\$1.5B |
		- **Major AI cloud contracts (sell-side comparison)**: the GCP–Anthropic deal (\$42.0B contract value, TPU v7) has an estimated year-1 EBIT margin of 37.5%, or 44.0% with 6-year depreciation. GB200/GB300-based neocloud deals for Microsoft, Meta and OpenAI (IREN, CoreWeave, Nebius, Nscale) are at ≈17–25%, and OCI–OpenAI (\$300B) at 37.1%.
		- **AI chip ownership, cumulative compute in H100 equivalents (2025Q4)**: Google ≈5.0M (≈3.8M of it TPUs), Microsoft ≈3.4M, Amazon ≈2.45M (≈1.0M Trainium), Meta ≈2.3M, Oracle ≈1.15M, China ≈1.1M (≈0.7M Huawei Ascend), xAI ≈0.55M, other ≈5.25M. Nvidia totals count sales from 2022 onward and TPU totals from 2023 onward.
	- ### Waymo — physical-world AI (BOND, citing YipitData)

		- Co-CEO Tekedra Mawakana (1/2025): Waymo is creating "an end-to-end, very, very robust, and large end-to-end system that's multi-modal in its foundation so that perception planning and prediction can become even more robust than it is today."
		- Co-CEO Dmitri Dolgov (3/2025): "What we've done in San Francisco is prove to ourselves – and to the world – that not only does autonomy work, but it works at scale in a market and can be a viable commercial product."
