- ## Roblox Read-Through
	- **Roblox screens expensive on EV/sales versus Japanese and Chinese publishers, but not out of range versus global growth / platform comps**:
		- Roblox: **6.1x / 5.0x / 4.2x EV/sales** for FY3/27E / FY3/28E / FY3/29E.
		- Global peer context:
			- EA: **6.2x / 6.1x / 5.9x**.
			- Take Two: **7.0x / 5.5x / 5.1x**.
			- Unity: **6.0x / 5.3x / 4.6x**.
			- Paradox: **5.9x / 5.5x / 5.2x**.
			- CD Projekt is the high-multiple outlier at **26.1x / 7.6x / 6.6x**.
	- **Roblox's multiple compresses visibly across the forecast window**: EV/sales moves from **6.1x FY3/27E to 4.2x FY3/29E**, implying the screenshot's estimates embed meaningful forward revenue growth.
	- **The table does not provide PE for Roblox**: Roblox is listed as **n.a.** on PE across all three forecast years, so this comp set is primarily useful for sales-multiple comparison, not earnings-multiple comparison.
	- **Scale context**: Roblox's **\$39.1B** market cap is below EA (**\$51.4B**) and Take Two (**\$45.9B**), but above Unity (**\$12.4B**) and all listed Japanese publishers except Nintendo and Sony.
- ## Segment Observations
	- **Japan publishers generally trade at lower EV/sales**:
		- Nintendo: **2.4x → 2.0x**.
		- Bandai Namco: **1.6x → 1.4x**.
		- Square Enix: **1.9x → 1.5x**.
		- Sony: **1.5x → 1.4x**.
		- Capcom and Koei Tecmo are the Japan high-multiple names at **5.1x / 4.6x / 4.2x** and **4.4x / 4.2x / 4.0x**, respectively.
	- **China peers sit between Japan publishers and global platform/growth peers**:
		- Tencent: **4.1x / 3.5x / 3.1x EV/sales**, **12.5x / 10.7x / 9.3x PE**.
		- NetEase: **3.4x / 3.1x / 2.9x EV/sales**, **14.2x / 13.2x / 12.5x PE**.
	- **Global video-gaming comps show the widest dispersion**:
		- Mature / scaled names such as EA still command premium EV/sales multiples.
		- Balance-sheet / turnaround names such as Ubisoft and Embracer trade at sub-1x EV/sales.
		- IP-cycle names such as CD Projekt can screen optically extreme in near-year EV/sales before normalizing sharply.
- ## Investment Questions for Roblox
	- Whether Roblox deserves to be valued closer to **global platform / growth comps** (EA, Take Two, Unity, Paradox) or closer to **traditional publisher comps** with lower sales multiples.
	- Whether the FY3/29E EV/sales compression from **6.1x to 4.2x** is justified by bookings / revenue growth, aging-up, ads, creator monetization, and Roblox Reality execution.
	- Whether the lack of PE support matters if investors remain focused on bookings growth, platform scale, and long-term operating leverage.
	- Whether Roblox's UGC platform dynamics and creator ecosystem warrant a structurally different comp framework than packaged-game publishers.
