Disclosure checks use Microsoft's [September 2026 10-Q, Note 1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/789019/000119312526436133/msft-20260930.htm), [FY2026 annual report](https://www.microsoft.com/investor/reports/ar26/index.html), and [FY2027 Q1 earnings release](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/investor/earnings/fy-2027-q1/press-release-webcast).

	- **Excerpt's argument**: OpenAI supplies a disproportionate share of Azure's incremental revenue. Separating compute purchases from revenue-sharing payments is essential to measuring that concentration.

Do not classify the entire commercial total as Azure compute.

- ## Profit: commercial earnings are not quantified here

	- The excerpt provides **no OpenAI-specific compute margin, allocated costs or operating-profit estimate**. The estimated revenue share is revenue, not automatically pure profit; neither it nor the commercial revenue total establishes OpenAI's contribution to Microsoft's operating income.
	- **Separate investment-accounting effect**: Microsoft's earnings release reports an OpenAI investment-related **\$207M net-income benefit and \$0.03 EPS benefit** in the September 2026 quarter, versus a **\$3.086B net-income reduction** a year earlier. These investment effects are separate from the commercial revenue analysis and do not measure profitability of serving OpenAI.
