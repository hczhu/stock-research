- ## LLM diffusion in biological research

	- **Source**: User-supplied *The Epoch Brief — November 11, 2026*, “How scientists are using AI”; underlying [Epoch AI analysis by Tara Abrishami, October 30, 2026](https://epoch.ai/data-insights/biorxiv-llms-vs-ai-tools).
	- **Epoch's finding**: Acknowledged LLM use has grown rapidly since 2023 and nearly reached the prevalence of specialized AI tools. Use spans literature review, writing, coding, and research/analysis.

- ## Adoption indicators: share of bioRxiv preprints

	- | Period | LLM use, any purpose | LLM research/analysis use | Specialized AI tools |
	  |---|---|---|---|
	  | September 2025 | 6.40% | 1.20% | 15.58% |
	  | January 2026 | 7.52% | 1.50% | 16.64% |
	  | September 2026 | 17.19% | 3.86% | 18.94% |
	- Source: Epoch's monthly table. Its newsletter rounds September 2026 to **17%, 4%, and 19%**, respectively; the research category is a subset of all LLM use, not an additional share.

- ## Where the tools are used

	- **General-purpose LLMs**: Research applications include experiment design, data labeling, and statistical analysis, alongside writing, coding, and literature review.
	- **Specialized AI**: Protein structure/design, image analysis, single-cell sequencing, and genomics. AlphaFold appears in about half of preprints acknowledging specialized tools.

- ## Measurement context

	- Epoch examines bioRxiv preprints using AI-assisted classification. Voluntary acknowledgments reflect both usage and disclosure norms; classification can be wrong, and undisclosed usage is not captured.
	- These are **paper-level disclosure rates**, not percentages of biotech companies, researchers, or paid customers. The source does not quantify commercial spending, productivity gains, or drug-development outcomes.
