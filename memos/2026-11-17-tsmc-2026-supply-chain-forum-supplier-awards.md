- ## TSMC's 2026 Supply Chain Forum: 35 Suppliers Honored
	- **Sources**: TrendForce News infographic "TSMC Honors 35 Suppliers at 2026 Supply Chain Forum," citing a TSMC press release dated Nov 17, 2026 (supplied by the user). I couldn't find the 2026 TSMC release online, so the category names below are TrendForce's abbreviations. The 2025 comparison is from [TSMC's 2025 forum press release](https://pr.tsmc.com/english/news/3274) (forum held Nov 25, 2025).
	- **Thesis (from the award lists)**: TSMC recognized **35 suppliers with 40 awards** in 2026, up from 30 suppliers and 33 awards in 2025. **ASML, KLA, Lam Research, TOK and ASM Taiwan each won two.** New categories cover global support, IT services and advanced-packaging production support. **14 of the 35 winners are Taiwan-based**, and 20 weren't on the 2025 list.
	- Related: [[TSMC-TSM-thesis]].

- ## Changes vs. the 2025 Awards
	- | Item | 2025 | 2026 |
	  | --- | --- | --- |
	  | Suppliers honored | 30 | **35** |
	  | Awards | 33 | **40** |
	  | Double winners | Applied Materials, ASML, SUMCO | **ASML, KLA, Lam Research, TOK, ASM Taiwan** |
	  | Categories | Production support; technology development and production support; green manufacturing; new fab construction; new fab construction safety; new fab automation | Same areas, plus **global support, IT services and advanced packaging production support** |
	- **Repeat winners (15)**: Advantest, All Ring Tech, Applied Materials, ASML, Austin Commercial, DISCO, EBARA, Jentech, KLA, Lam Research, Resonac, SCREEN, Shin-Etsu Handotai, SUMCO, Taiwan Puritic.
	- **Not on the 2025 list (20)**: Fujimi, TOK, ASM Taiwan, JX Metals, Lasertec, Namics, Taiwan Paper, TEL, V5 Tech, Dacin, GOSCO, Kedge, ABB, Leeming, Fengpei, Shiny Chemical, UIS, Daifuku, Pegatron, SPIL.
	- **2025 winners not on the 2026 list (15)**: Canon, Chang Chun Petrochemical, CSUN / G2C+ Alliance, Kokusai Electric, Onto Innovation, Sunlit, ULVAC, Asahi Kasei, JSR, Merck, China Steel Structure, Fu Tsu Construction, Marketech, TASA Construction, Murata Machinery.
	- **Advanced packaging**: SPIL's win is the only award for advanced-packaging production support, a category absent from the 2025 list.
	- **Automation**: Daifuku replaced Murata Machinery as the automation winner.
