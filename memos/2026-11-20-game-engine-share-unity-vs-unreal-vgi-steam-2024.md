- ## Game engines on Steam: Unity leads by count, Unreal leads by sales
	- **Source**: Joe Foley, "Unreal Engine dominates as the most successful game engine, data reveals (But Unity is still the most used)," Creative Bloq, February 13, 2025 (user-provided PDF). It reports a **Video Game Insights (VGI)** analysis of more than 13,000 games that sold at least 1,000 units on Steam in 2024. These are **Steam-only, 2024** figures, so they exclude consoles and mobile, where Unity is strong.
	- **Thesis**: Unity is the most-used engine, behind **51% of 2024 Steam releases**, but Unreal is the biggest by sales, with **31% of units sold vs. Unity's 26%**. Unreal's sales share jumped from 19% in 2023, and VGI expects it to reach **40% of annual sales by 2030** as large studios move off proprietary engines. Unity dominates the long tail of small games. Unreal wins where games get big.

- ## 2024 market share on Steam
	- | Engine | Share of games released | Share of units sold |
	  |---|---|---|
	  | Unity | **51%** | 26% |
	  | Unreal Engine | 28% | **31%**, the largest single engine |
	  | Godot | 5% | 1% |
	  | GameMaker | 4% | 1% |
	  | Ren'Py | 2% | 0% |
	  | Other, mostly proprietary engines such as Bethesda's Creation, Rockstar's RAGE and EA's Frostbite | 10% | 41–42%, **below 50% for the first time** |
	- **Proprietary engines are fading**: fewer than 10% of 2024 Steam releases used a developer's own engine.
	- **Little play time on new games**: only 15% of 2024 Steam play time went to new titles.

- ## Unity is strongest in small games, Unreal in large ones
	- Share of 2024 units sold, by game size:
		- | Game size (units sold) | Unity | Unreal | Godot and GameMaker | Other |
		  |---|---|---|---|---|
		  | Tiny (under 1K) | **50%** | 23% | 13% | 14% |
		  | Small (1K–100K) | **48%** | 27% | 6% | 19% |
		  | Medium (100K–1M) | **35%** | 32% | 5% | 29% |
		  | Large (1M+) | 22% | **31%** | 1% | **46%** |
	- **The pattern**: Unity's share falls steadily as games get bigger, while Unreal holds about 30% from small games up. In the largest games, proprietary engines still lead, but **Unreal beats Unity**.

- ## Share of Steam releases over time
	- | Year | Unity | Unreal | Other |
	  |---|---|---|---|
	  | 2012 | 9% | 17% | 71% |
	  | 2016 | 46% | 15% | 33% |
	  | 2020 | 46% | 21% | 27% |
	  | 2024 | 50–51% | **28%** | 13% |
	- Values for 2012–2020 are read from VGI's chart.
	- **Unity**: its share of releases has been flat at roughly 44–53% since 2016.
	- **Unreal**: it has gained every year since 2016, taking share from proprietary engines rather than from Unity.

- ## Unity vs. Unreal: relative strengths and weaknesses
	- | | Unity | Unreal Engine (Epic) |
	  |---|---|---|
	  | **Where it wins** | **Volume and the long tail**: half of all Steam releases and of games selling under 100K units. Roguelikes and turn-based strategy | **Sales and big games**: the largest single engine by units sold, ahead of Unity in 1M+ unit games. Action RPGs, soulslikes and FPS |
	  | **Momentum** | Flat share of releases since 2016. VGI expects Unity to **hold its sales level** by winning studios leaving custom engines | Sales share **19% → 31%** from 2023 to 2024, helped by **Black Myth: Wukong** and **Palworld** plus surprise indie hits. Big studios are moving over, for example Konami's *Metal Gear Solid Δ: Snake Eater* remake. VGI forecasts **40% of sales by 2030** |
	  | **Weaknesses** | Weaker in large, high-budget games. The article notes "recent upheaval at the company" | A Microsoft developer told the repo that Unreal's tooling is complete, but many real-time systems are heavy and only large teams can optimize them well, though each release gets faster ([[2026-03-28-microsoft-game-unity-unreal-engine]]) |
	  | **Indie pressure** | Godot and GameMaker are taking indie developers. Together they hold 13% of the tiniest games by units | Less exposed at the low end |
	- **Strategic context for Unity**: Unity's business is no longer only the engine. Its Grow segment monetizes ads with the **Vector** model and runtime data from games built on Unity. Runtime-data opt-in on new projects is over 90%. Strategic Create (engine) revenue was \$165M in Q3 2026, +11% YoY ([[U-2026-Q3]]).
