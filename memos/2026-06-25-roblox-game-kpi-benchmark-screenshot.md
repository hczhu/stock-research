- ## Roblox Thesis Implications
	- This snapshot supports the idea that Roblox creator analytics are becoming more sophisticated: creators can benchmark not only raw visits but retention, playtime, payer conversion, and payer quality against platform distributions.
	- The data also illustrates why Roblox's monetization ceiling depends on experience quality and funnel design, not just platform traffic. A game can be top-decile on engagement while still under-monetizing if payer conversion is weak.
	- For Roblox as a platform, the attractive pattern is that high-ARPPU games can exist even when conversion is low. If discovery increasingly rewards long-retention / high-quality games, better funnel tooling and creator education could convert engagement into bookings over time.
