- ## Daytona's CEO: Smaller Server Providers Now Ask for Upfront Payment on Bulk CPU Orders
	- **Source**: Ivan Burazin (@ivanburazin), X post, 17:22 on Nov 16, 2026, with replies. From a screenshot the user supplied, when the post had **4.8K views, 64 likes and 18 bookmarks**. The post URL wasn't included. Burazin is co-founder and CEO of [Daytona](https://thenextweb.com/news/ivan-burazin-daytona-ai-agent-sandboxes-tnw-podcast), which runs sandboxes for AI agents, so the company buys general-purpose compute at volume.
	- **Thesis (Burazin's view)**: the CPU shortage is reaching smaller buyers. Single cloud instances are still easy to get. Bulk orders of hundreds of servers from smaller providers now come with upfront-payment demands.
	- Related: [[2026-09-18-cpu-shortage-agents-cloud-capacity]] attributes the shortage to agent workloads. [[2026-09-21-expert-call-cpu-shortage-gpu-overbuild-correction]] offers a more deflationary read.
- ## What Burazin Reports
	- "You can feel the CPU shortage starting."
	- **Small orders**: "You can still spin up a single EC2 instance."
	- **Bulk orders**: "But try ordering 200, 500, or 1,000 servers from smaller providers and they're suddenly asking for upfront payments."
	- **Not stated**: which providers, how large the upfront payments are, and whether prices or lead times also changed.
