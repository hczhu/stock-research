- ## Piper Sandler's Fall 2026 Teen Survey: Brand, App and Media Winners and Losers
	- **Source**: Clark Schultz, "Winners and losers from Piper Sandler's survey of teen spirit," Seeking Alpha News, Oct 13, 2026, text supplied by the user. The article summarizes Piper Sandler's semi-annual survey of teen spending and media trends. It doesn't give the sample size or most mindshare percentages.
	- **Thesis (survey's findings)**: Teen spending is up 6% YoY, led by upper-income teens. Mindshare is shifting from Nike, Adidas and Lululemon toward On and specialty retailers. TikTok gained app mindshare while Snapchat lost it, and YouTube gained viewing time from Netflix and Prime Video. Roblox usage improved, and GTA 6 has strong launch intent.
	- Related: [[Roblox-RBLX-thesis]] and [[2026-03-28-pew-us-teens-ai-chatbot-usage-2025]].
- ## Spending
	- Teens reported spending **up 6% YoY** this fall, and upper-income teens **up 11%**.

- ## Energy Drinks
	- More teens drink energy drinks than a year ago, and they drink more of them: **1.54 a week vs. 1.39** a year ago.
	- **Brands teens plan to drink**: Monster 23%, Alani Nu 20%, Red Bull 18%, Celsius (CELH) 16%.
- ## Apps, Video and AI
	- **Favorite app**: TikTok, **+2 points** of mindshare. Instagram (META) second and flat. Snapchat (SNAP) third, **−3 points**.
	- **Video time**: YouTube (GOOGL) gained teen usage time at the expense of Netflix (NFLX) and Amazon Prime Video (AMZN).
	- **AI use**:
		- | LLM / AI product | Share of teens using it |
		  | --- | --- |
		  | ChatGPT (OpenAI) | 66% |
		  | Gemini | 35% |
		  | Google AI Overviews | 32% |
		  | Google AI Mode | 30% |
- ## Gaming
	- **GTA 6 (TTWO)**: about **32% of all teens** intend to buy it immediately at launch, rising to about **50%** among teens who own a console.

- ## Roblox: teen usage across survey waves

	- **Additional sources**: User-supplied historical figures, added November 17, 2026; survey partner DECA's [Fall 2024 results](https://www.decadirect.org/articles/deca-and-piper-sandler-complete-48th-semi-annual-survey) and [Spring 2025 results](https://www.decadirect.org/articles/deca-and-piper-sandler-complete-49th-semi-annual-survey); [Fall 2026 secondary coverage](https://www.roic.ai/news/piper-sandler-teen-survey-tiktok-roblox-and-beauty-lead-as-snapchat-and-netflix-lag-10-13-2026). The original Seeking Alpha summary only said usage improved; the following figures supplement it.
	- | Survey wave | Active Roblox usage | Never played Roblox | Used to play, no longer does |
	  |---|---|---|---|
	  | Spring 2024 | 34% | 22% | 44%, per supplied summary |
	  | Fall 2024 | 46% | 17% | Not supplied |
	  | Spring 2025 | 42% | 17% | Not supplied |
	  | Fall 2026 | 49%, supplied and secondarily reported | Not supplied | Not supplied |
	- **Reported trend**: Active usage rose **15 percentage points** between Spring 2024 and Fall 2026, with an intervening decline from Fall 2024 to Spring 2025. Never-played share fell **5 points** by Fall 2024 and was unchanged in Spring 2025. Fall 2026 is the highest of these four observations; Fall 2025 and Spring 2026 are not included in the supplied series.
	- **Frequency**: The user's Fall 2026 summary describes strength in daily and weekly activity but gives no percentages. Active usage includes different playing frequencies and is not a daily-active-user penetration figure.
	- **Survey scope and verification**: These are U.S. teen survey responses, not Roblox's global user metrics or a longitudinal retention study. Fall 2024 surveyed 13,515 teens (average age 15.8); Spring 2025 surveyed 6,455 (average age 16.2). Those ages describe survey samples, not Roblox's user base. The 2026 secondary article itself says the 49% figure was not verified against the official report; the full Fall 2026 Roblox table and daily/weekly breakdown remain unverified here.
