- ## Hyperscaler capex — Goldman & BofA estimates (CY20–28E)
	- **Source**: Two sell-side exhibits (user-provided images). (1) Goldman Sachs Global Investment Research, "Hyperscaler capex CY2020–27E" ($bn), per-company bars for AWS/Microsoft/Google/Meta/Oracle plus the summed total. (2) BofA Global Research, "Updated Capex Estimates for Alphabet, Meta and Amazon AWS" ($bn) — Updated vs Previous for 2026E/2027E/2028E, i.e. an estimate **raise**.
	- **Thesis**: Both banks are marking hyperscaler capex sharply higher into 2027–28. Goldman has the big-5 total going from **$406B (C25) → $740B (C26E) → $996B (C27E)** — roughly doubling in two years and ~13.5x the CY20 level. BofA's data point is the *direction of revisions*: it raised Alphabet/Meta/AWS out-year capex across the board (Meta 2028E +23%, AWS 2028E +25%). The read-through is unambiguously positive for the AI infrastructure supply chain — memory/HBM, foundry, networking, and data-center compute — which is where most of the portfolio's semi exposure ([[$DRAM]], [[$SOXX]], [[$TSM]], MediaTek) and infra software ([[$DDOG]], [[$NET]]) sits. The risk is that these are *estimates* of *spending*, not returns — a capex cycle this steep raises the digestion/ROI question.
-
- ## Bottom line
	- Consensus among the two banks: the AI capex curve is still steepening, not flattening. Goldman's total capex **nearly doubles C25→C27E (+145%)** and BofA is *raising* rather than trimming out-year numbers — the opposite of a cycle rolling over.
	- **Oracle is the standout new entrant**: Goldman has ORCL capex going $2B (C20) → $35B (C25) → $95B (C27E), ~47x over the period and the fastest grower — it is now modeled as a genuine 5th hyperscaler (Stargate/OpenAI-driven).
	- **The biggest *upward revisions* are in the out-years** (2027–28E), where visibility is lowest. BofA's Meta and AWS 2028E numbers rose ~23–25% in a single update — a sign estimates are chasing announced spend, and a reminder the tail years carry the most model risk.
	- For the portfolio: this supports the long memory/semi thesis and the hyperscaler equity longs ([[$GOOG]], [[$META]], [[$AMZN]]), but it is the **money going in**, not coming out. Pair with the ROI/digestion skepticism in [[2026-06-18-ai-bubble-debate-capital-cycle-cyclicality-podcast]] and [[2026-06-10-morgan-stanley-hyperscaler-capex-accounting-risk]].

- ## Risks & caveats
	- **Estimates, not actuals** — especially 2027–28E; the largest upward revisions sit in the least-visible years, and sell-side numbers are chasing announced spend.
	- **Spending ≠ returns**: a 2.45x capex ramp in two years is exactly the setup for an ROI/digestion air-pocket if AI revenue lags. See the bubble/capital-cycle skepticism in [[2026-06-18-ai-bubble-debate-capital-cycle-cyclicality-podcast]] and accounting/depreciation risk in [[2026-06-10-morgan-stanley-hyperscaler-capex-accounting-risk]].
	- **Definitional noise**: Goldman "AWS" vs BofA "Amazon AWS" vs total-Amazon capex differ; Oracle/Meta include large non-AI facilities. Treat magnitudes as directional, not precise.
	- **Numbers read off bar-chart labels** — cross-checked against the printed totals (Goldman total reconciles to component sum within rounding).
