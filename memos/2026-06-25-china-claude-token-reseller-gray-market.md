Links cited: a reseller [yunwu.ai/pricing?provider=Anthropic](https://yunwu.ai/pricing?provider=Anthropic) and a ChinaTalk piece "How to buy cheap Claude tokens." **Unverified single-source claim** — treat fraud/volume specifics as allegation, not established fact.
- **Thesis**: A large gray market resells Claude access in China at 70–90% (up to 93%) below official API prices. It distorts perceived China model economics, is partly funded by fraud, and — most relevant for investors — allegedly **resells Claude outputs & reasoning chains to Chinese labs**, tying directly into the Anthropic–Alibaba "illicit extraction"/distillation dispute.

- ## What's Happening
	- Chinese resellers offer **Claude tokens 70–90% below official Anthropic API prices**.
	- How they source the cheap capacity:
		- **Reselling pooled Claude Max 5x subscription capacity** (sub arbitrage vs metered API).
		- **Payments fraud.**
		- **Reselling model output & reasoning chains to various Chinese labs.**
	- Example: one reseller (yunwu.ai) lists **Opus 4.8 at a 93% discount** to official API rates.

- ## Why the Channel Exists
	- **Claude and ChatGPT are both blocked in China** — need a VPN, and **can't pay with a Chinese bank card**.
	- So most Chinese users who want Claude **buy via a reseller** — the easiest and cheapest access path to Anthropic models in China.

- ## Anthropic's Countermeasure
	- Resellers allegedly run **tens of thousands of bot accounts**.
	- This is cited as a reason **Anthropic introduced identity verification** — to slow the onslaught of bot accounts.

- ## Knock-on: China Model Pricing
	- Claimed to be **one reason DeepSeek & GLM are priced so cheaply** — they compete against "impossibly low" gray-market Claude token prices and must keep prices low for adoption.
	- Implication: headline cheapness of Chinese models may be **partly a response to subsidized/fraudulent Claude access**, not purely a cost-structure advantage.

- ## Why It Matters (Investment Implications)
	- **Distillation / IP leakage**: reselling reasoning chains to Chinese labs is a direct vector for training competitor models on Claude outputs — corroborates the Anthropic↔Alibaba extraction narrative and is a real moat-erosion risk for frontier labs.
	- **Revenue leakage for Anthropic**: Max-plan capacity arbitrage + payments fraud cannibalize official API revenue and distort usage signals (cf. OpenRouter token-share data — China model usage may be flattered by this dynamic).
	- **Trust & safety cost**: identity verification and anti-bot measures are now a structural cost of doing business; friction for legitimate users.
	- **China access reality**: blocks + payment rails mean Western models reach China mostly through unofficial channels — relevant to any TAM assumption about US labs monetizing China.
	- **Watch-items**: enforcement actions / account crackdowns, whether reseller prices compress after ID verification, and any hard evidence on the distillation claim from the Alibaba case.

- ## Caveats
	- Single HN comment; the fraud, "tens of thousands of bot accounts," and reasoning-chain-resale claims are **not independently verified** here.
	- Reseller discount figures are advertised rates, not audited.
