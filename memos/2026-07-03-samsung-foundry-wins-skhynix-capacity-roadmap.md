Meanwhile SK Hynix is escalating its capacity roadmap (3× wafers by 2034) and NAND layer count — reinforcing the [[DRAM-memory-ssd-index-thesis]] view that supply cannot catch demand for years.

- ## Samsung Foundry: AI Customer Wins Stacking Up

	- The capacity crunch at TSMC is handing Samsung foundry the marquee clients it has struggled to win — a potential turnaround for its loss-making foundry division.

	- | Customer | Deal | Node / Detail |
	  |---|---|---|
	  | **Anthropic** | Preliminary talks for **custom AI server chip** manufacturing | **2nm** process + advanced packaging. Anthropic (\$965B) named Samsung/SK Hynix/Micron "strategic infrastructure partners" in Series H. Hired **Clive Chan** (ex-OpenAI custom-chip team, Broadcom "Jalapeño" inference chip). Chip is for **inference**, not training (Nvidia still dominates training). |
	  | **Google** | I/O die for 10th-gen **TPU ("Icefish")** | Samsung 2nm for **I/O die**; TSMC 1.4nm for compute die (split-manufacturing). ~2028 mass production. |
	  | **Tesla** | **AI6** chip | **\$16.5B (KRW 25T)** contract, Texas fab |
	  | **Groq** | LPUs (language processing units) | Already in production, deployed on Nvidia platforms |
	  | **Nvidia** | HBM + next-gen foundry | Jun 8 Jun Young-hyun ↔ Jensen Huang talks expanded to **HBM5 (2027)** + next-gen Groq; Samsung already shipping **HBM4 for Vera Rubin** |
	  | AMD / BYD | Future CPUs / autonomous-driving chips | In talks (dual-sourcing vs TSMC) |

	- **Strategic read**: Hyperscalers/AI labs are adopting **dual-sourcing** (splitting orders TSMC + Samsung) to de-risk supply. Only 3 firms can make advanced logic (TSMC, Samsung, Intel); with TSMC 2nm/1.4nm sold out, Samsung is the default alternative. Samsung 2nm GAA yield reportedly **>60%** (Q1 2026), approaching the ~70% economic threshold; 2nm-related orders projected **+130% in 2026**.

- ## SK Hynix: Capacity & NAND Roadmap Escalation

	- **DRAM wafer capacity**: now targeting **3× wafer output by 2034** (up from prior "double in 5 years") — Yongin expansion accelerated ~a decade. \$67B budgeted 2024–2028 for expansion. Chairman Chey: "moving as fast as we can," but supply stays tight for years.
	- **NAND**: mass-produce **375-layer 3D NAND by year-end 2026**; first to introduce **molybdenum** word-line metal gate (replacing tungsten used >10 yrs — Lam Research calls Mo the only viable path for high-layer 3D NAND). Follow-ups: **480 / 604-layer**. Racing Samsung/Kioxia to the **400-layer** frontier.
	- **HBM4 equipment**: ordered **Hanmi TC Bonder 4.5 "Griffin"** (thermo-compression bonding), first Griffin contract, **KRW 44.2B (\$28.7M)** — capacity buildout for Nvidia demand. Added ASMPT + Hanwha Semitech as rival bonder suppliers.
	- **HBM share**: **56.4%** in Q1 2026 (from F-1 filing) — still #1 despite the DDR5-margin pivot.
	- **F-1 filing detail**: purchasing **26 ASML NXE3800E EUV scanners**; disclosed the antitrust class-action as a litigation risk. Nasdaq ADR raising **~\$29.4B (up to 17.79M new shares)** for capacity — proceeds explicitly for fabs/packaging/tools.

- ## Memory-Adjacent Component Signals

	- **Samsung UFS 5.0** (industry-first, on-device AI): **10.8 GB/s read / 9.5 GB/s write** (vs UFS 4.1's 4.3/4.1) — built on 9th-gen V9 V-NAND; mass production Q4 2026. Feeds the on-device-AI storage upgrade cycle (Galaxy S27).
	- **Silicon capacitors (Samsung Electro-Mechanics)**: market growing **>18%/yr**; Semco emerging as major Si-Cap supplier for AI servers alongside Murata/TSMC; MLCC + FC-BGA + Si-Cap now a full AI-power-component lineup. Confirms the LTA/tightness dynamic is **spreading beyond memory** into passives and substrates.

- ## Corporate / Market Context

	- **Anthropic ↔ Alibaba friction**: Anthropic accused Alibaba of exploiting its models; Hang Seng hit a 1-yr low — a geopolitical undercurrent to the China-AI-supply-chain story.
	- **KOSPI 9,000** first crossing (Jun 18) driven by Samsung + SK Hynix (48% of index); violent swings (−10% Tuesday, +5% Thursday) — analysts frame as **correction within a bull run, not a bubble top** (KB Securities). Bloomberg meme-stock warning stands.
	- **Samsung buyback**: expected **KRW 89T (\$64.5B)** over 3 years (~3× the prior decade's total) — helped it reclaim the market-cap crown from SK Hynix.

- ## Watch Items / Risks

	- Anthropic/Google/Tesla wins are **early-stage or 2028-dated** — execution and yield risk on Samsung 2nm before revenue materializes.
	- SK Hynix's 3×-by-2034 capacity is a **long-dated** buildout ("wait two more US elections") — near-term supply stays tight, but it signals the eventual supply response that could end the cycle in the early 2030s.
	- Molybdenum NAND transition and 400-layer+ stacking carry yield/warpage risk (see HBM high-stack reliability issues in [[2026-07-03-dram-hbm-supercycle-news-roundup-jun-jul-2026]]).
