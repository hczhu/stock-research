- ## ChatGPT user growth and usage across search, images and voice
	- **Source**: [Alex Kantrowitz's interview with Adam Fry, ChatGPT consumer product lead](https://www.bigtechnology.com/p/openai-chatgpt-search-is-about-20), Big Technology, November 3, 2026. Usage figures are OpenAI's as reported in the interview.

- ## Reported usage
	- | Metric | OpenAI's figure | Scope |
	  |---|---|---|
	  | ChatGPT weekly active users | 1.2 billion, up from 1 billion in about 60 days | Increase of 200 million weekly active users, or 20% from the starting base; not paid subscribers |
	  | Search-like queries | Roughly 20% of Google's query volume | OpenAI's comparison of query volumes, not a search market-share estimate |
	  | Image generation | 1.5 billion images per week | Counts images created, not people using the feature |
	  | Voice usage | 250 million weekly active users | Combined figure for dictation and speaking with ChatGPT; not a standalone count for conversational voice |

- ## Search behavior and product direction
	- Fry described usage expanding beyond writing and coding into lookups that depend on current information, such as people, places, finance and health.
	- The public interview excerpt does not establish how OpenAI counts search-like queries within a multi-turn conversation. The ratio does not establish how ChatGPT ranks against Bing, whose figure in the interview was framed as share of the search market.
	- Fry said OpenAI plans to merge Chat and Work modes and remove the toggle between them.
