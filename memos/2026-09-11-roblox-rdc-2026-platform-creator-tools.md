- ## RDC 2026 — Roblox expands beyond the app
	- **Source**: Roblox, [“RDC 2026: The World Needs More Play”](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/09/rdc-2026-the-world-needs-more-play) (September 11, 2026); Isa Muhammad, [PocketGamer.biz conference summary](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/roblox-unveils-new-play-creation-and-monetisation-tools-at-rdc-2026/) (September 14, 2026). The user supplied the latter article; Roblox's post provides the primary-source wording and metric dates.
	- **Thesis**: RDC 2026 broadens Roblox from an in-app, predominantly 3D multiplayer platform toward a cross-surface game-development and distribution layer. The investment question is whether easier access, AI-assisted creation, discovery, and faster creator payments increase durable engagement and bookings enough to offset higher infrastructure, payout, and safety costs.
	- This is an initial RDC memo; subsequent conference material can be added to the relevant sections below.
- ## Scale and creator economics
	- | Signal | Roblox disclosure | Investment context |
	  |---|---|---|
	  | Engagement | **29 billion hours** in the last quarter across **180+ countries** | Establishes the audience scale available to new games and distribution formats; it is not itself evidence that RDC features have lifted usage. |
	  | Cumulative creator earnings | **More than \$5 billion** since DevEx launched in 2013 | A large payout pool helps attract professional developers, but is also a cost to Roblox. |
	  | Recent creator earnings | **Approximately \$1.7 billion** in the 12 months through June 30, 2026 | More relevant to the current creator-business opportunity than the cumulative figure; the report gives no RDC-specific incremental payout forecast. |
	  | Roblox Plus incentives | **More than 300 million Robux** earned by creators in the first four months, through August 17 | Early evidence that subscription-linked incentives are reaching developers; Robux is not directly comparable with dollar payouts without conversion and eligibility context. |
	  | Build creator funnel | **About 9,000 games published**; **71%** of game creators had never used Studio, through September 1 | Prompt-based creation is bringing in new makers, but published-game count does not establish quality, retention, or monetization. |
- ## Distribution and game formats
	- **Roblox Everywhere** is a planned option for creators to publish standalone apps on mobile, PC, and consoles while Roblox supplies the underlying technology and services. Roblox gave no exact launch date, storefront terms, or unit-economics detail.
	- **Chrome play without an install** is planned by year-end 2026 from a game's Experience Details Page, with other browsers to follow. Links from search, video, social media, and messaging could convert interest into play with less friction; conversion and incremental traffic remain unproven.
	- **Offline solo play**, alongside a live multiplayer mode, is targeted for mid-2027 after early access. New orthographic-camera, sprite-sheet, animated-image, and UI support broadens Roblox into 2D and puzzle games, not just 3D social worlds.
	- Turn-based asynchronous notifications are scheduled for Studio Beta in October 2026 and full release by year-end. Together with offline play, they could extend session occasions beyond synchronous multiplayer.
	- The cross-platform strategy may enlarge Roblox's addressable game catalog and acquisition funnel, but it also raises an unanswered question: how Roblox captures economics when a creator's game is discovered and played outside the core app.
- ## Creation, discovery, and social loops
	- **Build** is a mobile-first, prompt-based game creation tool expanding public alpha from New Zealand to Serbia and Singapore. Roblox added an asset library, desktop access, iterative controls, analytics, and monetization; mobile game advertising is planned.
	- **Scene Generator** is planned for later in 2026 to turn text prompts and reference images into functional scenes across Build and Studio. Lower production friction should increase supply; the bottleneck then shifts toward game quality, discovery, and live operations.
	- PocketGamer.biz also reports new NPC behaviors, including NPCs that can playtest. If effective, AI could reduce both creation and testing costs; the article provides no adoption or performance data yet.
	- **Moments**, Roblox's gameplay-video discovery surface, is fully available in the United States subject to age and regional limits. Videos from game-detail pages are due to appear there; avatar-item purchases from videos are planned for October, and Build games are intended to become playable within Moments later.
	- Roblox says its ranking system now considers differing audience patterns: shorter games for younger players and deeper, repeat-play games for older users, looking out to **28 days and beyond** rather than only the first week. It is also testing whether a game brings new players to Roblox as a ranking signal.
	- Friends chat across games, voice typing, and policy-reviewed preset “Quick Words” aim to strengthen the social graph while keeping communication moderated. These features may support retention, but the company has not quantified their effect.
- ## Creator cash flow and platform control
	- **Roblox Wallet** is planned for eligible U.S.-based independent creators aged 18 or older later in 2026. Once a creator opens a Wallet, cleared earnings are paid in real currency each business day; international expansion is planned for the following year. U.S. payment services are provided by Airwallex.
	- **Roblox Card**, connected to Wallet, is planned for 2027. Faster access to earnings could make Roblox a more attractive operating platform for creator businesses, but Roblox has not disclosed the card's economics or whether it changes creator retention.
	- The integrated stack—engine, hosting, discovery, social graph, monetization, and payouts—could deepen creator dependence on Roblox even as standalone distribution reduces dependence on the Roblox app interface.
- ## Interpretation and unresolved points
	- The clearest near-term signals are the creator funnel and payout scale; most headline RDC features are announcements or limited rollouts, not yet demonstrated revenue drivers.
	- More cheaply produced games could improve variety or flood discovery with weak content. The 28-day ranking and Moments experiments are therefore as important as the creation tools.
	- Browser and standalone distribution expand Roblox's moderation and child-safety perimeter. The economics of off-app acquisition, platform fees, identity, and policy enforcement remain unspecified.
