- ## Competitive Dynamics: MediaTek vs Qualcomm
	- **Divergent strategies**:
		- **MediaTek** — concentrates *all* resources on **ASIC** (custom AI accelerators).
		- **Qualcomm** — **customization + standardization in parallel**, covering both AI accelerator *and* CPU.
	- Industry view: MediaTek is **not at a disadvantage** as long as it (a) stays focused on ASIC and (b) holds **Google long-term** as anchor.
	- Google order quality is the differentiator: once a mass-production schedule is set, the year's incremental revenue is largely pinned down, and at meaningful scale — i.e. **more reliable earnings visibility** than other CSPs.

- ## The Meta Angle
	- Meta's cloud-AI silicon strategy described as **"chaotic/unclear"** — in-house chip plans repeatedly adjusted; multiple CPU partners and approaches.
	- Meta appears to **multi-source deliberately** (Broadcom + MediaTek both in play for accelerators; Arm + Qualcomm for CPU) — "develop products with as many partners as possible."
	- Landing Meta would be **"no small help"** to MediaTek's ASIC growth, but is **upside, not the base case**; Google underpins the base case.

- ## Investment Implications
	- **MediaTek ([[$2454.TW]])**: multi-generation Google TPU pipeline (Zebrafish/Humufish/Triggerfish v9) gives a **multi-year revenue ramp through 2029**; a Meta win would add a second leg toward/above $10B. Reinforces the "MediaTek = AI-infra company, not just a handset chip company" thesis.
	- **Earnings-visibility premium**: Google's aggressive, schedule-locked ordering means MediaTek's ASIC revenue is more *forecastable* — supports a re-rating on visibility, not just TAM.
	- **Meta ([[$META]]) multi-sourcing**: bullish for the *merchant custom-silicon complex* broadly (both **Broadcom** and **MediaTek** can win at Meta) — Meta diversifying away from single-vendor dependence; watch for share splits by generation.
	- **Broadcom ([[$AVGO]])**: incumbent Meta AI-accelerator partner; MediaTek entry = potential share pressure / second-source dynamic, though Meta's "many partners" stance can grow the pie for both.
	- **Qualcomm ([[$QCOM]])**: Dragonfly + $15B/2029 target makes cloud AI a genuine new leg; head-to-head with MediaTek in ASIC while also chasing CPU — broader but less focused than MediaTek.
	- **Upstream readthrough**: more merchant-ASIC programs ramping 2026–2029 = sustained demand for advanced packaging, HBM, and foundry (TSMC) — consistent with the broader AI-infra / memory-tightness thesis.

- ## Caveats
	- Supply-chain rumor; MediaTek won't confirm. Meta as "second customer," codenames, and the "$10B only a matter of time" framing are **reported claims**.
	- Revenue targets ($10B MediaTek implied, $15B Qualcomm 2029) are aspirational, not guided run-rates.
	- Meta's shifting strategy means partner allocations could change by generation.
