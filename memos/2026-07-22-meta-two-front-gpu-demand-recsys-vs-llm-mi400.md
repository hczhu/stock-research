- **Thesis frame (the useful insight)**: Meta has **two distinct GPU-demand fronts with opposite hardware requirements**, and the SA-vs-practitioner debate is really about which one Meta should optimize for. **(1) 搜广推 / recsys** (search, ads, recommendation — Meta's core money engine): the workload that is **actually GPU-short right now**, and it wants **memory-bandwidth-per-dollar + a higher CPU:GPU ratio + *less* total HBM/compute**. **(2) LLM (training + inference)** at Meta Superintelligence Labs (MSL / "TBD Lab", Alexandr Wang): wants **maximal HBM + compute**, and general LLM GPUs are **poorly suited to recsys**. Meta's custom AMD MI400 is a deliberate bet on front (1) — which is why SA calls it a "disaster for LLM" while practitioners call SA wrong ("现在就是搜广推缺卡" — it's recsys that's short on cards).

- ## The Meta Custom AMD MI400 (as SA describes it, per the chat)
	- | Spec | Standard MI455X | **Meta custom MI400** |
	  |---|---|---|
	  | Relative die size | 1× | **~½** |
	  | HBM capacity | 432 GB | **144 GB (~⅓)** |
	  | HBM stacks | 12× HBM4 **12-Hi** | **6× HBM4 8-Hi** (config cut in half) |
	  | Optimization target | general (LLM train/infer) | **recsys** — cost + memory-bandwidth-per-unit |
	- **Design rationale (SA)**: Meta's recsys infra team wants to **tune the CPU-compute : GPU-compute ratio** to better fit recommendation workloads and **optimize cost per unit of memory bandwidth** — hence the smaller die, ~⅓ the HBM capacity, and thinner 8-Hi stacks.
	- **SA's bear framing**: the SKU may be **fine for recsys but poor for LLM inference/training**; worse, the decision was made **before TBD Lab (MSL) existed**, so **Alexandr Wang's team had no input**, and because compute + HBM are both cut, the SKU is **less attractive to TBD Lab engineers** (a talent-recruiting negative). Headline: "billions wasted / disaster-level mistake."

- ## The Practitioner Pushback (the chat's counter)
	- **"SA is wrong" / SA傻逼**: the cut-down custom chip is *fit-for-purpose*, not a blunder. **"现在就是搜广推缺卡"** — right now it is **search/ads/recommendation that is GPU-constrained**, i.e., Meta's core revenue engine, not LLM.
	- **General LLM GPUs are the wrong tool for recsys**: "那些卡的 GPU/CPU 比都不适合搜广推" — the high-GPU/low-CPU, HBM-maximal ratio of LLM cards is **mismatched to recsys**, which is memory-bandwidth-bound and needs more CPU-side compute.
	- **Why rent LLM GPUs to Anthropic** (the user's question, "为啥 zuck 还要租 gpu 给 anthropic?"): precisely *because* those LLM-optimized cards are **not ideal for recsys anyway** — so leasing that capacity out (the compute-toll-booth from [[2026-07-13-semianalysis-meta-superintelligence-1yr-update]]) is rational while Meta builds custom recsys silicon for its core workload.
	- **The open question the chat lands on**: does LLM actually help Meta's core business? "市场明明还在等 LLM 帮到 Meta 核心业务" — the market is *still waiting* for LLMs to lift the money engine. How? "AI-generate 广告" (AI-generated ads) **and** "搜广推质量" (search/ads/rec *quality*). That is the crux: recsys silicon monetizes today; LLM's contribution to the core is still prospective.

- ## Investment Read-Throughs
	- **$META — the two-front demand is the key structural insight**: Meta must provision **two different kinds of silicon at once** — cost/bandwidth-optimized **custom recsys accelerators** (the MI400) for the workload that *is* GPU-short and directly monetizes (ads/rec, where small model gains compound over **trillions of impressions**, per [[2026-07-10-sharp-tech-meta-ai-ads-zuckerberg-strategy]]), **and** general HBM-heavy GPUs for **MSL/LLM**. This reconciles the seemingly contradictory moves: **build cut-down custom chips for recsys** *and* **lease/own general GPUs** (some rented to Anthropic). It's vertical-integration-for-your-highest-ROI-workload — the Meta analog of [[2026-07-20-google-frozen-v2-gemini-in-silicon-inference-chip]] (Google specializing silicon for its core). Bull: Meta optimizes spend to the actual bottleneck. Bear (SA): if the custom SKU is mis-scoped and LLM matters more than assumed, billions are stranded and MSL talent is deterred.
	- **$AMD — a marquee win, but lower content per chip**: Meta custom MI400 is a flagship AMD design win, yet it's **~half die + ⅓ HBM** vs the standard part. Read: AMD lands **large recsys volume at Meta** but at **reduced ASP/HBM content per unit** vs a full MI455X — good for AMD's AI-accelerator footprint and Meta relationship, less good for per-chip dollar content. Also a datapoint that **hyperscaler custom SKUs fragment the merchant roadmap** (Meta bending AMD to a recsys-specific config).
	- **HBM / memory ([[$MU]], SK Hynix, Samsung; [[DRAM-memory-ssd-index-thesis]]) — two-sided**: the custom recsys SKU **cuts HBM content roughly in half (6× 8-Hi vs 12× 12-Hi; 144 vs 432 GB)** — a caution that **not all AI silicon is HBM-maximal**; recsys accelerators want **bandwidth, not capacity**, so they use fewer/thinner stacks. BUT recsys is still **memory-bandwidth-bound** (it wants HBM bandwidth, just less capacity), and the volume is large, so it's incremental HBM demand at a **lower-capacity mix**. Net: bullish HBM *bandwidth* demand, but a reminder that **capacity-per-accelerator is workload-dependent** — the recsys leg dilutes the "every AI chip carries 400GB+ HBM" assumption.
	- **MSL / talent ([[2026-07-13-semianalysis-meta-superintelligence-1yr-update]])**: SA's point that the MI400 decision **pre-dated TBD Lab and excluded Wang's team**, reducing the SKU's appeal to LLM engineers, dovetails with the MSL memo's flagged **infra-org culture friction** (a compute-team hire already quit). A concrete example of the org seams between Meta's legacy recsys-infra org and the new MSL.
	- **The "搜广推缺卡" signal**: Meta's **core ad/rec business is currently compute-constrained** — a real, present bottleneck (not a future-LLM story). This both **justifies aggressive capex** on the money engine and explains **why Meta simultaneously rents out LLM GPUs** it can't optimally use for recsys. Watch whether Meta's ad-performance/rec-quality gains (the monetization payoff) show up as ARPU/engagement lift.
