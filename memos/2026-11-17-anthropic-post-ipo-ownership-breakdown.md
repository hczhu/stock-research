- ## What the S-1 Says About Control
	- **Class F (co-founders)**: one share held by Founder LLC, the vehicle for the seven co-founders, carries a **fixed 50.1%** of voting power on ordinary stockholder matters. The co-founders decide by majority how it is voted. Class F has no economic rights.
	- **Class A**: one vote per share, capped at **49.9%** of total voting power in aggregate. The co-founders also vote their own Class A shares individually.
	- **Class N**: no vote, and held "primarily" by strategic business partners. It converts to Class A when sold or transferred to an unaffiliated third party.
	- **Class T**: one share held by the Long-Term Benefit Trust, which elects the Class T directors, who will be a majority of the board.
	- **Class E**: to be issued to employees (not co-founders). It votes only on limited matters and has no economic rights.
	- **Reconciling 61.3%** (*my math*): 50.1% from Class F, plus 49.9% × the co-founders' share of voting Class A. That implies the co-founders hold about **22%** of voting Class A. Their equity share is lower because Amazon's and Google's stakes are largely non-voting.
- ## Cross-Checks Against the S-1
	- **Co-founder holdings**: the S-1 lists each co-founder's Class A shares as of Sep 30, 2026, between **231.8M** (Sam McCandlish) and **244.2M** (Tom Brown), about **1.69B** in total. Against the ~**16.9B** fully diluted share base implied by the Public Benefit Fund grant (below), that is about **10%** (*my math*), below the 13% in the breakdown. The breakdown doesn't give its share base, so the gap is unexplained.
	- **Public Benefit Fund**: Anthropic contributed **846.8M Class N shares**, 5% of fully diluted shares as of June 30, 2026, and the board approved a framework for issuing more at its discretion. The breakdown's 6.5% is above that initial 5%.
	- **Co-founders are selling**: all seven are listed as selling stockholders, but the S-1 leaves the number of shares offered blank.
- ## Implied Valuation
	- *My math*: if **\$26B** buys **1.7%** at the IPO price, Anthropic's implied post-IPO equity value is about **\$1.5T**. The rounding of 1.7% moves that by roughly ±\$45B.
	- At that value, the **6.3%** sold to IPO buyers would be roughly **\$95B** of stock, primary and secondary combined.
	- These are inferences from the breakdown's rounded percentages, not disclosed figures.
	- **The chip and cloud partners**: Nvidia, AMD, Broadcom, Micron, Microsoft and TSMC are all compute suppliers or partners. Their combined \$26B at the IPO price adds them to Amazon and Google as strategic shareholders.
