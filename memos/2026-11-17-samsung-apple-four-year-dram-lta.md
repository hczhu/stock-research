- ## Samsung and Apple Near a Four-Year DRAM Long-Term Agreement
	- **Source**: screenshot of an English-language report supplied by the user, citing "semiconductor industry sources on the 17th" (Nov 17, 2026). The underlying report is from Korea's Maeil Business Newspaper, per [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/11/17/apple-rare-long-term-memory-deal-samsung/) and [Wccftech](https://wccftech.com/apple-abandons-short-term-dram-purchasing-strategy-samsung-contracts-2027/). Neither company has confirmed the deal.
	- **Thesis (report's view)**: Apple, which usually favors short-term contracts and price cuts at each renewal, is close to a multi-year DRAM LTA with Samsung. The deal guarantees a minimum volume but leaves price to future market conditions. An industry insider calls it "a highly unusual deal—one rarely seen."
	- Related: [[2026-11-03-stratechery-apple-memory-purchase-commitments-consumer-memory-market]] (Apple's purchase obligations rose \$33B from June to September; press reports put Samsung at ~60% of Apple's DRAM). - ## Why the Report Calls It Unusual
	- Apple "is known for using its 'superbuyer' status to squeeze suppliers as much as possible in its contracts." It "tends to favor short-term contracts and demand price cuts each time they are renewed."
	- Industry insider: "Given Apple's usual practices, its LTA with Samsung is a highly unusual deal—one rarely seen."
