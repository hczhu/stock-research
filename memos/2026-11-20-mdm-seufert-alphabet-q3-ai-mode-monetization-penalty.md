- ## Alphabet Q3 2026: AI Mode's monetization penalty (MDM)
	- **Source**: Eric Seufert, "Alphabet Q3 2026 earnings: AI Mode's monetization penalty," MDM (Mobile Dev Memo), October 29, 2026 (user-provided PDF). Full call notes: [[GOOG-2026-Q3]].
	- **Thesis**: Seufert considers the first chatbot bear thesis on Google refuted: Search engagement is not migrating to chatbots, and query growth is the fastest in five years. The live question is the second thesis, that **AI Mode keeps queries but monetizes them worse**. Seufert compares it to **Meta's Reels**, which dragged on revenue early and turned "net neutral" about a year after Meta acknowledged the drag. Optimizing a new ad format takes time and traffic, so a short-term revenue hit is a deliberate strategy, not proof the bear case is right.

- ## Q3 2026 advertising results
	- | Line | Q3 2026 revenue | YoY growth | Q2 2026 growth |
	  |---|---|---|---|
	  | Total advertising | \$82.0B | **+10.6%** | +14% |
	  | Search | \$63.4B | **+12.0%** | +17% |
	  | YouTube ads | \$11.2B | **+9.2%** | +12.9% |
	  | Network | \$7.5B | **+1.4%**, the first YoY increase since Q2 2022 | −0.7% |
	  | Subscriptions, platforms and devices | \$14.3B | +11.0% | +15.2% |
	- **Search lost its majority**: Search was **49.7% of total revenue**, down from 52.8% in Q2, the first quarter in Alphabet's modern history where it was not a majority. Cloud revenue more than doubled, lifting total revenue growth to 25%.
	- **Market reaction**: Alphabet missed on revenue and EPS. The EPS miss was mainly a loss on its SpaceX stake. The stock fell as much as 10%.
	- **Management's reasons for the Search slowdown**: FX (the largest sequential factor), the EU ending its de minimis customs exemption, and AI Mode's weaker monetization. Seufert focuses on AI Mode because the other two are not unique to Google, and Meta's ad revenue grew 26% in the same quarter.
	- **Context**: 12% Search growth is still healthy and above the two-year low of 10% in Q1 2025.

- ## Two chatbot bear theses
	- | Bear thesis | Claim | Seufert's verdict |
	  |---|---|---|
	  | **1. Usage migrates** | Queries move from Search to chatbots, leaving less to monetize | **Essentially refuted** |
	  | **2. Monetization erodes** | Google keeps queries through AI Overviews and AI Mode but monetizes them worse | **More defensible**, but not settled |
	- **Evidence against thesis 1**:
		- Query growth was "the highest it has been in the last five years" in Q3, after an all-time high in Q2.
		- **AI Overviews has 2.5B MAU and AI Mode 1B MAU**. In Seufert's words, Google has become a chatbot "engagement sink."
		- **Follow-up queries count**: the objection that conversational follow-ups inflate query counts is "mostly pedantic." Follow-ups are engagement and can be more monetizable, because commercial intent sharpens as a conversation goes on, which is OpenAI's "multi-turn" pitch too.
		- **Cue's Safari remark is dated**: Eddy Cue's April 2025 claim that Safari searches fell for the first time in 22 years came at the low point of a deceleration cycle. It was also part of antitrust testimony about Google's more than \$20B a year in payments to Apple.
		- **Timing**: if migration did not happen from Q2 2025 to Q2 2026, with ChatGPT at 1.2B weekly users, it is "intellectually dishonest" to claim it suddenly started now.
	- **Evidence for thesis 2**: Philipp Schindler said on the call that "strong growth in AI Mode queries had an impact because of the lower monetization we were seeing in AI Mode." Google says the **"majority of AI Mode queries are incremental,"** which implies a minority are cannibalizing traditional Search and having their monetization deliberately sacrificed.
	- **Google is leaning in**: Google is scaling ads in AI Overviews and AI Mode to more users and queries, taking AI Mode ads international for the first time, and launching multi-turn ads in the US. Seufert expects the share shift toward the lower-monetizing format to continue, and faster.

- ## The Meta Reels precedent
	- **Timeline**: Reels came to the Facebook app in 2022 and expanded in May 2023. Reels monetized below Feed and Stories but generated more ad impressions, so early on it cannibalized higher-paying impressions and slowed revenue growth.
	- **Zuckerberg on the Q2 2022 call**: "the faster that Reels grows, the more revenue that actually displaces from higher-monetizing surfaces," but slowing Reels "would be worse for our products and business longer term."
	- **Q3 2023 call**: Meta called Reels **"net neutral to overall company ad revenue."** Seufert reads that as Reels still monetizing below Feed, with its larger impression volume offsetting the lost Feed and Stories ads.
	- **Seufert's lesson**: traffic has to be pushed into new formats to generate the data that optimizes them. Whether AI Mode's gap is permanent or can be closed through ad placement, ad load and targeting is unknown.

- ## Other notes
	- **Network's first growth since 2022**: Network rose \$101M YoY in Q3 after falling \$51M in Q2. That could be noise on small numbers. Seufert's alternative explanation: as Search click-through rates fell with more AI Mode ads, Performance Max, which automatically optimizes for conversions, **routed more spend to Network placements** (AdSense and AdX) to hit advertisers' targets.
	- **YouTube**: ad growth slowed to 9.2%, and subscriptions, platforms and devices slowed to 11.0%. Seufert's model puts advertising at **just under two-thirds of YouTube revenue**.
