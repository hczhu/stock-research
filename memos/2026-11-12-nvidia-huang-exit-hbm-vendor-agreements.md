		- Lee Jae-lim, "[Jensen Huang's exit clouds future Nvidia demand for Korean chipmakers](https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/business/jensen-huangs-exit-clouds-future-nvidia-demand-for-korean-chipmakers/12970507)", Korea JoongAng Daily, Nov 12, 2026.

	- **Thesis**: HBM supply already under contract is well insulated. Capacity is sold out years ahead under long-term agreements backed by prepayments, and detailed planning runs through Nvidia's working-level executives rather than Huang. The risk is to **future purchase decisions and chairman-level deal-making**, and SK hynix carries more of that risk than Samsung because Nvidia is a much larger share of its business.
- ## Event
	- On **Nov 12, 2026**, Huang resigned with immediate effect as president, CEO and board member. CFO **Colette Kress** became interim CEO, and former Synopsys CEO **Harvey Jones** became Nvidia's first board chair. \$NVDA closed down **4.34%** that day.
	- Nvidia gave no official reason. Media reports point to affairs and an alleged foreign-intelligence effort targeting Huang. The board opened an independent investigation and said it had no reason to believe security or company secrets were compromised.
	- No permanent successor has been named. Speculated candidates include Ian Buck, Kress, and **SVP Jeff Fisher**, who is also the memory vendors' working-level contact. Q3 FY27 results are due **Nov 17**.

- ## What Holds and What Is Exposed
	- **Likely to hold**:
		- Both vendors build their detailed business plans with Nvidia executives such as **Jeff Fisher**, not with Huang personally. Officials expect existing supply agreements to be unaffected.
		- Prepayments make the LTAs harder to unwind, and capacity sold out through 2027–2029 leaves little room for volumes to be renegotiated quickly.
	- **Exposed**:
		- **Chairman-level ties**: Huang had close relationships with Samsung's Lee Jae-yong and SK's Chey Tae-won. One official: "If Huang is removed, it could inevitably affect networking among top industry executives… We cannot rule out the possibility that business discussions could slow or existing plans could change."
		- **Future volumes and strategy**: the uncertainty sits in Nvidia's *next* round of memory purchases and its AI investment strategy under new leadership. Roadmap disruptions or regulatory changes would complicate the vendors' own capex plans.
		- **Huang-era Korea commitments** (memo inference: these chairman-level deals are the most exposed to the "networking" risk):
			- About **260,000 GPUs** to Korea by 2030, announced October 2025: 50k each to Samsung, SK Group and Hyundai Motor Group, 60k to Naver Cloud, and about 50k to the government.
			- A ₩1.34T investment in Naver.
	- **Offsets**:
		- Mirae Asset (Kim Young-gun) says the bigger risk is **tighter US export controls or sanctions**, not damage to Nvidia's competitiveness. If share does shift, AMD benefits first, then Broadcom and Marvell.
		- Rising custom-ASIC demand gives memory makers **more bargaining power** with Nvidia.
		- An industry source called Huang's departure "a temporary distraction".
