- **Thesis frame**: FDE is the model companies' **"阳谋" (open/overt strategy — a plan that works even when everyone sees it coming)**: send engineers to help enterprises deploy agents (→ **sell tokens**), **sediment the enterprise's tacit knowledge into "Skills,"** then **internalize those Skills back into the model**. Each deployment simultaneously grows near-term consumption *and* feeds the long-term moat. The uncomfortable corollary: once the knowledge is captured, **the enterprise needs fewer people** — efficiency gains flow either to business expansion (best case) or to headcount cuts (base case). AI-literate software engineers get a **temporary reprieve** by being the ones who do FDE work.

- ## The Model-Company Strategy (the "阳谋")
	- **Step 1 — Land & sell tokens**: FDEs embed in the customer, make the agent actually work in the customer's messy real workflows, and drive metered token consumption. FDE is the wedge that converts a hard enterprise sale into live usage.
	- **Step 2 — Capture knowledge as Skills**: the enterprise's domain judgment, exception handling, and workflow context get **structured into reusable Skills / knowledge bases** during deployment.
	- **Step 3 — Internalize into the model**: those Skills are **folded back into the model**, so the capability that once required an FDE + the customer's experts becomes native model capability.
	- **The flywheel for the lab**: every deployment (a) grows current revenue (tokens), (b) accretes proprietary workflow data that (c) makes the *next* deployment cheaper and the model stronger — a self-reinforcing loop where **each project pays for the next**. This is the same mechanism as RL-environments capturing real white-collar work ([[2026-07-13-semianalysis-meta-superintelligence-1yr-update]]): FDE is a *human-in-the-loop RL-environment-generation engine* pointed at a paying customer.

- ## The Labor / Macro Read (the pessimistic near-term)
	- Once workflow knowledge is captured and modeled, **the enterprise needs fewer people**. Two outcomes: (a) efficiency → **business expansion** = win-win; (b) no expansion → **cost-cutting / layoffs ("降本增效")**. The commentary's base case leans (b).
	- **Every industry gets "rolled" (卷) by AI**; AI-literate software engineers merely **"苟延残喘" (eke out a reprieve)** for a while *because* FDE work still needs them — until the Skills they build make even that redundant.
	- **The paradox**: the *stronger* AI capability gets, the *more pessimistic* the near-term feels — "the future is bright, but the transition is brutal." Directionally consistent with the [[2026-07-13-benedict-evans-token-pricing-commodity-infrastructure]] value-capture-vs-value-creation tension and the enterprise "everyone is busier, not less" field note ([[2026-04-13-enterprise-ai-agents-field-notes]]).

- ## The Practitioner Playbook — "make every project work for the next"
	- The single highest-value FDE practice: **turn every engagement into structured, reusable assets** so the delivery system compounds rather than the individual.
	- Sediment **all** of it into structured data: **each client conversation, requirement judgment, delivery plan, reason for every revision, and final outcome.**
	- Once there are enough cases, convert them into the **Agent's knowledge base, Skills, and evaluation standards**.
	- **The operating loop**: new client's first conversation ends → **Agent produces a v1 baseline plan** → team **calibrates judgment, handles exceptions** → new experience **flows back** into the knowledge base.
	- **The payoff**: once the flywheel turns, the firm's most important asset is **no longer a star employee but a continuously compounding delivery system**. Its value isn't turning people into agent-executors — it's letting **every ordinary member call on the best person's experience and judgment.** "People churn, but organizational capability doesn't reset to zero."

- ## Investment Read-Throughs
	- **Frontier labs (Anthropic / OpenAI)**: FDE / "Applied AI" teams are a **structurally advantaged GTM + data-moat engine** — the answer to "how do labs escape commoditization" from [[2026-07-13-benedict-evans-token-pricing-commodity-infrastructure]]. It converts services revenue into (a) token consumption and (b) proprietary Skills that deepen the model moat. Watch **FDE headcount growth** and any disclosure of Skills/marketplace as a lab strategy — a tell that the capture-and-internalize loop is being productized.
	- **[[$PLTR]] (the FDE originator)**: Palantir invented the forward-deployed-engineer model; the AI-lab adoption **validates the playbook** but also means **the labs are now running Palantir's motion pointed at the model layer**. Double-edged: confirms FDE-as-moat, but raises the question of whether general-purpose model Skills eventually commoditize bespoke FDE-heavy deployment (Palantir's differentiation is ontology + gov/regulated depth, harder to internalize).
	- **AI-services / SI firms & the "delivery-system" flywheel**: the practitioner playbook is a blueprint for **defensible AI-services businesses** — value accrues to whoever builds the compounding **delivery system (knowledge base + Skills + evals)**, not to individual consultants. Favors firms that instrument and productize their engagements; bearish for undifferentiated body-shop SIs whose knowledge walks out the door.
	- **Enterprise software incumbents**: the capture-Skills-then-internalize loop is the mechanism by which **workflow knowledge migrates from the customer (and from point SaaS tools) into the model layer** — the substrate-shift risk behind the Starbucks-insourcing ([[2026-07-09-starbucks-ai-insourcing-enterprise-software]]) and "agents as the new interface" themes. Incumbents with proprietary, hard-to-observe workflow data + distribution resist it; thin-wrapper apps don't.
	- **Token demand / compute**: FDE deployments are a **direct token-consumption growth channel** (land → run agents in production) and, via always-on/agentic workflows, a durable inference-demand driver — reinforcing [[2026-07-16-dylan-patel-podcast-ai-infra-memory-cpu-optics-power]] and [[2026-07-18-napkin-llm-inference-cost-serving-economics]].
	- **Labor / macro caution**: base-case labor displacement ("降本增效") is a **demand-quality risk for consumer-exposed and labor-heavy sectors** and a political/regulatory overhang (ties to the AI-liability and gating themes in [[2026-07-17-ai-liability-agents-google-visa-carrier-publisher]]).

- ## What to Monitor
	- Frontier-lab FDE / Applied-AI headcount and whether Skills get productized into a marketplace (the internalize-and-resell step).
	- Whether enterprise deployments show **efficiency → expansion** (win-win) or **efficiency → headcount cuts** (the base case) — the swing factor for the macro read.
	- Palantir's positioning as labs run the same motion: does ontology/regulated-depth stay un-internalizable, or does it commoditize?
	- Emergence of AI-services firms that visibly compound a **delivery system** (knowledge base + Skills + evals) vs. those still selling individual expert hours.
