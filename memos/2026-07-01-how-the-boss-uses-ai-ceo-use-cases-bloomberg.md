- **Source**: Bloomberg Businessweek, "THE CEO DIET — How the Boss Uses AI," by Kate Krader (Pursuits). 12 executives describe how they personally use AI. Anecdotal/qualitative — signal on *executive-level* AI adoption and tool mindshare, not quantitative data.
- **Thesis**: AI has crossed from IC/analyst tooling into **daily CEO workflows** — as a sounding board, chief-of-staff, data-query layer, and meeting-prep engine. Two investment-relevant signals: (1) **Claude/Anthropic is named more than any other tool** by these executives, and (2) **agentic** setups (teams of AI agents, background daily agents) are showing up in the wild, not just in vendor demos.

- ## Cross-Cutting Patterns
	- **AI as chief-of-staff, not notetaker** — the recurring framing (Naficy explicit; Kaplan's agent team; Mares's background agent). Value is orchestration/prioritization, not transcription.
	- **Sounding board / decision friction reducer** — Pross, Salis, Muyco, Gozney all use it to think faster and decide with less friction; all stress judgment stays human.
	- **Prompting sophistication** — meta-prompting (Olanrewaju) and "is this your best work?" (Blumberg) show executives learning technique, not just typing questions.
	- **Personalized agents on own corpus** — MattBot (Blumberg) = fine-tuned/RAG on a person's lifetime of content; a repeatable "digital twin" pattern.
	- **Meeting prep** is a killer app — auto-briefs on people and companies (Naficy, Mrkšić).
	- **Displacement signals**: BI/analyst reports (Snyder) and **presentation decks** (Snyder: "it killed" decks) — direct threat to the analyst-report and slideware workflow.

- ## Investment Implications
	- **Anthropic / Claude mindshare**: Claude is the **only named LLM** in the piece — cited by 2 of 12 by name (Naficy "Claude Cowork," Blumberg "Claude agent") with none naming ChatGPT/Gemini. Small sample, but consistent with Claude's strength in high-value knowledge work and the OpenRouter usage data we track (Anthropic models over-index in coding/agentic use). Bullish read on Anthropic's enterprise/prosumer position; Anthropic is private, so the readthrough is to its stack/backers (Amazon, Google) rather than a direct ticker.
	- **Ecosystem tools normalized into C-suite workflows**: **Granola** (AI notes), **Slack** ([[$CRM]]) and **Gmail/Workspace** ([[$GOOGL]]) named as the substrate agents read from → integration surface matters; the winners are whoever the agents plug into.
	- **Agentic is real at the top**: teams-of-agents (Kaplan) and background daily agents (Mares) validate the agent narrative behind [[$MSFT]] Copilot agents, Salesforce Agentforce, etc. — demand signal for agent orchestration platforms.
	- **Productivity-software risk**: "AI killed my presentation decks" and "skip the analyst report" are warning shots for slideware and traditional BI dashboards — watch [[$MSFT]] PowerPoint, and BI/analytics vendors, as generative + NL-query erodes the deck/report workflow (double-edged: same vendors also ship the AI replacing it).
	- **Horizontal, not vertical**: use cases span finance (Allianz), consulting (McKinsey), CPG (Acme), fitness (CityPickle), healthtech (TrueMed), hardware (Gozney) — reinforces AI as a broad-based productivity layer, supporting the aggregate-demand thesis for compute/memory ([[$NVDA]], DRAM) upstream.
