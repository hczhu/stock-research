- ## MoffettNathanson: YouTube is the "New King of All Media," worth up to \$550B
	- **Source**: Lucas Manfredi, "YouTube Could Be Worth \$550 Billion as Analyst Crowns Platform 'New King of All Media'," TheWrap, March 31, 2025 (user-provided PDF), reporting a MoffettNathanson research note from the same day. All figures are as of early 2025. Revenue and margin numbers are MoffettNathanson estimates, because Alphabet discloses YouTube economics only periodically.
	- **Thesis**: MoffettNathanson values YouTube as a standalone business at up to **\$550B**, nearly 30% of Alphabet's value at the time. It expects YouTube to pass Disney as the largest media company by revenue in 2025 and calls YouTube **"significantly under-monetized"** on the TV screen relative to its reach. It projects operating margin expanding from 14% to 18% by 2027, mainly through better subscription economics.

- ## Valuation
	- **Method**: enterprise value as a multiple of 2024 revenue, benchmarked against media and tech peers:
		- | Peer | EV / 2024 revenue |
		  |---|---|
		  | Netflix | 10.5× |
		  | Meta | 8.8× |
		  | Roku | 2.4× |
		  | Warner Bros. Discovery | 1.4× |
		  | Fox | 1.3× |
		  | Disney | 1.3× |
	- **Implied multiple**: \$550B on \$54.2B of 2024 revenue is about **10.1×** (memo calculation), close to Netflix's multiple.

- ## Financial estimates
	- | Year | Revenue | Operating income | Operating margin |
	  |---|---|---|---|
	  | 2024 | **\$54.2B**, #2 media company behind Disney | \$7.8B (estimate) | 14% |
	  | 2025E | About \$64B (memo calculation from income ÷ margin) | \$10.2B | 16% |
	  | 2027E | About \$77B (memo calculation from income ÷ margin) | \$13.8B | 18% |
	- **Growth drivers**: subscription revenue keeps outgrowing advertising on subscriber additions. Total revenue grows in the low-to-mid double digits from 2025 to 2027.
	- **What the estimates leave out**: no further price increases, so subscription growth slows as net additions moderate, especially for YouTube TV.

- ## Market opportunity and engagement
	- **Addressable market (US)**: the **\$85B consumer pay-TV market** and the **~\$30B streaming market excluding Netflix**. MoffettNathanson sees YouTube as "the central aggregator for all things professional video."
	- **Pay-TV leadership**: it projects YouTube TV reaching a **10% share of pay-TV spend** and becoming the industry leader by the end of 2026, as traditional distributors decline. That would strengthen its hand in content negotiations and create a pricing opportunity.
	- **Subscribers (early 2025)**: YouTube TV had **8M+ subscribers**, the fourth-largest pay-TV provider after Comcast, Charter and DirecTV, and the largest virtual (streaming) pay-TV provider. YouTube Music and Premium had **125M+ subscribers** worldwide, including trials.
	- **TV viewing (Nielsen, February 2025)**:
		- YouTube took a record **11.6% of US TV viewing time**, passing Disney.
		- Time spent watching YouTube on TV was up **53%** from two years earlier. Viewing by adults 65+ rose **96% YoY**, nearly doubling in two years.
		- | Age group | Share of YouTube viewing on TV |
		  |---|---|
		  | 2–11 | 16.9% |
		  | 12–17 | 6.9% |
		  | 18–34 | 21.0% |
		  | 35–49 | 19.4% |
		  | 50–64 | 20.4% |
		  | 65+ | 15.4% |
	- **Recommendations**: build a **skinnier YouTube TV bundle** of broadcast, sports and news channels plus streaming services such as Hulu, Paramount+, Peacock and Max, an opportunity "ripe for the taking." Alphabet should also give **greater disclosure** on YouTube's economics.
