- ## Datadog’s Largest Customer: OpenAI Renewal and Spending Timeline
	- **Thesis**: Datadog renewed its largest customer even as usage fell. The account still spent above its contractual minimum in Q3 2026, but Datadog assumed only that minimum in Q4 guidance.
	- **Source**: Datadog earnings calls and SEC filings, published analyst estimates, and reporting cited in the table. Dates are when the information was disclosed, not necessarily when the spending occurred.
	- **Attribution**: Datadog leaves the customer unnamed in the contract and guidance disclosures. Stifel, Truist, and Bernstein identify it as OpenAI. The Q3 call did not explicitly say OpenAI received better pricing.

- ## Contract Values, Revenue, and Growth
	- **The two nine-figure disclosures are not comparable annual spend figures.** The 2025 call disclosed an annualized expansion. The 2026 call disclosed a renewal without specifying its term or whether the value was annualized.
	- **No confirmed customer revenue share or contractual floor.** Datadog’s guidance with and without the account measures its effect on growth, not its share of revenue. Cohort contributions, analyst revenue estimates, and ARR estimates are also distinct measures.
