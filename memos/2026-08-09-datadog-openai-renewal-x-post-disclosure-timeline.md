- ## What Changed in the Narrative
	- **Spending estimate → migration forecast → renewal and above-minimum consumption.** These statements come from different sources and periods. The older prediction of a complete exit remains unconfirmed by the later company statements.
	- The Datadog thesis still describes an expected Q4 2026 full migration as a risk. That entry should be read as the earlier forecast preserved in the repository; the Q3 disclosures establish continued spending at that point but do not settle the eventual migration outcome.
	- **Amounts remain distinct.** The 2025 research forecast, the 2025 annualized expansion, and the 2026 renewal are different measures. They cannot be added together or treated as successive disclosed annual spending totals.
	- **Remaining gap** — The collected company disclosures do not reveal the largest customer's exact 2026 revenue, minimum commitment, or 2027 usage. The source of the original research exhibit is also unidentified.
