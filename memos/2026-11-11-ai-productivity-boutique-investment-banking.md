- ## How a boutique investment bank used the time AI saved
	- **Source**: The author's conversation with a former coworker who is now a VP at a boutique investment bank. The VP described his own group's experience.
	- **Thesis**: Faster work on individual tasks gave the group capacity to pursue more revenue and do more for clients. Analyst hours stayed roughly flat as the group expanded the amount of work it took on.

- ## What the VP saw
	- AI use had spread within the group compared with a few months earlier. Associates and VPs could use Claude to arrange logos on a deck or maintain an investor contact log with minimal effort. Both tasks previously took analyst time.
	- | Role | How the group used the extra capacity |
	  |---|---|
	  | Analysts | Senior bankers staffed them on more deals at the same time |
	  | Vice presidents | Asked for more pages and deeper analysis in client materials |
	  | Directors | Assigned new work, including maintaining tear sheets and comparable-company sets for niche verticals |
	  | Managing directors | Pursued new mandates more often |
	- The VP described the result as more capacity to pursue new business and deliver better, faster work to existing clients. The conversation gave no figures for won mandates or realized revenue.

- ## What the example says about AI productivity
	- **More work was worth doing once it got cheaper.** Keeping niche comp sets current and adding analysis to a pitch can be worthwhile when each task takes less time. Claude also let senior bankers handle some routine work themselves, freeing analyst capacity for deals and research.
	- **For banking economics**, the revenue opportunity depends on whether more pitches convert into mandates and whether faster delivery helps retain clients or execute more deals. Analyst hours alone would miss those channels.
	- **For enterprise AI demand**, the VP's account points to adoption across seniority levels and to new workflows becoming practical. The potential usage comes from the extra work the group chooses to do as well as the tasks it already did.
	- **Author's read**: The conversation reinforced my skepticism of broad AI job-loss predictions. I suspect the same pattern can appear in other industries where teams have work they would take on if they had the capacity. This account gives one group's experience with task time, staffing, and hours. It offers no headcount trend or industry-wide evidence.
