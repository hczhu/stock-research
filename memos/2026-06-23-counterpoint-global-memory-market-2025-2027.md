- ## Bear Case: Supply Glut and Price Correction Risk

	- The article headline explicitly flags two risks alongside the bullish forecast:
		- **Supply glut risk**: The magnitude of the demand surge is attracting aggressive capacity expansion. If AI infrastructure capex decelerates before new fab capacity is absorbed, the supply/demand balance could flip.
		- **Price correction possible from H2 2027**: Counterpoint's own base case includes a potential price correction beginning in the second half of 2027 — the same year the market is projected to peak at 2,100조 원. This is consistent with the classic memory cycle pattern: demand-driven ASP expansion eventually attracts oversupply, and the correction follows with a lag.
	- This framing (2027 as both the market peak and the inflection into potential correction) is the central tension for memory equity positioning into 2027.
