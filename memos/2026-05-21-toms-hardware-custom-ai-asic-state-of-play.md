- ## Custom AI ASICs as of May 2026
	- **Source**: Luke James, Tom's Hardware, published May 21, 2026. The attached PDF contains the opening two pages. The original online post supplies the company sections, and company disclosures clarify selected figures. This is a May 2026 snapshot. [Original post](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/semiconductors/custom-ai-asics-examined-from-broadcom-to-mtia)
	- **Thesis**: Custom silicon was forecast to take a larger share of AI server shipments while GPU shipments still grew. Hyperscalers can optimize chips for workloads they run at scale. Broadcom can sell design and networking alongside those chips, and TSMC can supply fabrication and packaging across competing designs.

	  | CoWoS allocation | NVIDIA **about 60%** (~595K wafers), Broadcom **15%** (~150K), AMD **11%** (~105K) | Article estimates. Monthly capacity and allocation totals may use different periods or assumptions |
	- **Revenue follows shippable systems.** Securing leading-edge wafers is one step. Packaging slots, HBM, networking, and power availability can each delay the deployment of a complete cluster. TSMC can benefit from both architectures where it supplies the node or package, while the pace of expansion and mix of products determine how much of that demand converts to revenue.
