- ## NYC Office “Phantom Vacancy”: Useful Signal, Faulty Crash Thesis
	- **Source**: User-provided screenshots of a January 1, 2026 Reddit post by u/Drewthinkalot, “Is NYC Commercial Real Estate about to crash? I found a massive ‘Phantom Vacancy’ problem”; checked against the [live Reddit post](https://www.reddit.com/r/RealEstate/comments/1q1hndc/is_nyc_commercial_real_estate_about_to_crash_i/), official ENERGY STAR methodology, NYC records guidance, Q2 2026 Manhattan market reports, and public-company filings.
	- **Thesis**: Low energy use can help identify buildings with little physical activity, but it cannot establish false lease occupancy, undisclosed forbearance, or a market-wide crash. Subsequent evidence refutes the broad NYC crash call: Manhattan demand and landlord operating metrics improved through mid-2026. The narrower credit warning remains valid for obsolete, overleveraged offices, as national office CMBS delinquency reached 11.91% in July.

- | Claim | Screenshot evidence | Assessment |

- ## Why the Method Does Not Prove the Thesis
	- **Lease occupancy is not desk attendance**: [ENERGY STAR defines office occupancy](https://portfoliomanager.energystar.gov/pm/glossary) as the share of floor area leased and occupied; it explicitly says not to count empty cubicles or employees entering the property. A tenant paying for lightly used space can therefore produce high lease occupancy and low energy use without anyone falsifying a rent roll.
	- **The occupancy field is weak evidence**: ENERGY STAR says the entered occupancy value does not affect the score or other metrics, cannot be tracked historically in Portfolio Manager, and may be entered as a best guess when certification is irrelevant.
	- **EUI is not a headcount meter**: It divides annual energy by floor area. Weather, operating hours, HVAC zoning, renovations, electrification, tenant equipment, server migration, energy retrofits, and mixed-use composition all affect it. ENERGY STAR reports a very wide EUI distribution even among offices.
	- **The comparison needs controls**: A valid model would use weather-normalized energy, office-only peer groups, consistent gross floor area, fuel mix, operating hours, worker density, water use, lease expirations, and building-level time series. “Comparable to a vacant warehouse” is not a controlled benchmark across property types.
	- **2019 is a structurally different baseline**: Hybrid work intentionally lowers daily attendance while long leases remain enforceable. NYC’s own February 2026 forecast said office attendance had recovered to only **63% of its pre-pandemic baseline** in December 2025.
	- **ACRIS silence is not loan-status proof**: [ACRIS](https://www.nyc.gov/site/finance/property/acris.page) is a property-document system. The absence of a new mortgage or UCC filing does not establish missed payments, maturity default, or a private forbearance agreement. Those require loan documents, servicer data, court filings, or lender confirmation.
	- **No outcome variable is shown**: The post gives no default rate, foreclosure rate, subsequent vacancy, or comparison with an unflagged control group. It cannot demonstrate predictive accuracy or estimate market prevalence.

- ## Prediction Audit Through Mid-2026
- | Observable result | Subsequent evidence | Verdict on crash call |
  |---|---|---|
  | Manhattan availability | CBRE reported **14.4%** in Q2 2026, down **310 bps year over year** | Tightening supply contradicts an imminent broad vacancy shock |
  | Leasing and absorption | Q2 leasing was **7.88M sq. ft.**, 24% above the five-year quarterly average; H1 net absorption was **+5.08M sq. ft.** | Demand improved after the post was published |
  | Asking rents | CBRE reported **$80.17/sq. ft.**, up 4% year over year | Market pricing did not show a broad crash |
  | Longer demand trend | Colliers called H1 2026 Manhattan’s strongest first half since 2002 and reported availability at **13.0%** | Independent market methodology points in the same direction |
  | Office credit | Trepp reported **11.91%** office CMBS delinquency in July 2026 | Distress is real, but concentrated credit impairment is not equivalent to a citywide operating collapse |
	- **Verdict**: The market-wide crash prediction is refuted so far. The better model is a bifurcated recovery: scarce, well-capitalized, high-quality Manhattan offices improve while weaker buildings and legacy loans continue to default, extend, convert, or sell at losses.

- ## Public-Company Read-Through
	- **[[$SLG]]**: Q2 2026 Manhattan same-store cash NOI rose **4.3%** year over year; signed replacement rents increased **18.0%**; occupancy including signed leases reached **94.7%**, versus 93.0% at year-end 2025. These audited operating disclosures directly contradict a portfolio-wide phantom-vacancy thesis, though “signed but not commenced” still differs from physical attendance. [SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1040971/000162828026049346/a26q2earningsrelease.htm)
	- **[[$VNO]]**: New York office occupancy was **92.2%** and same-store office cash NOI rose **6.2%** year over year in Q2 2026. The portfolio is benefiting from quality and location rather than tracking the weakest private Class B/C properties. [SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/899689/000089968926000048/vno-063026xxex991xearnings.htm)
	- **[[$ESRT]] and [[$BXP]]**: The same analytical distinction applies: portfolio quality, lease rollover, tenant credit, concessions, and cash NOI matter more than citywide foot traffic or raw EUI.
	- **Lenders and CMBS holders**: The post is more relevant at the individual collateral level than as a macro short thesis. Energy and water time series may improve surveillance when combined with debt-service coverage, maturity dates, appraisal reductions, special-servicer status, taxes, and verified lease rolls.

- ## Durable Insight
	- “Phantom vacancy” is best understood as **utilization risk**: tenants may keep paying for space they barely use, creating future downsizing risk at lease expiry.
	- The investable variable is the timing and concentration of lease rollover relative to debt maturity—not whether a building feels empty on a given day.
	- A repeatable alternative-data signal would report precision, recall, false positives, realized defaults, and excess returns against a matched control sample. Until then, the Reddit analysis is a promising screening hypothesis rather than evidence of systemic fraud or an imminent NYC office crash.
