- ## Steam: Revenue, Users, Releases and Market Share
	- **Sources** (two PDFs supplied by the user):
		- Richard Jaimes, "Steam Game Statistics", Quantumrun Foresight, Dec 22, 2025. It cites SQ Magazine and Backlinko.
		- Naveen Kumar, "Steam Statistics (2026) – Active Users & Market Share Data", DemandSage, Aug 20, 2026. It cites Alinea Analytics, PC Gamer, SteamDB and Statista.
		- Valve is private and doesn't publish financials, so every revenue figure is a third-party estimate. The two sources disagree on several numbers, as noted below.
	- **Thesis**: Steam's gross sales hit a record \$11.1B in 1H 2026, up 14.5% YoY, and Alinea puts 2025 at about \$20B. Steam holds 74–75% of PC digital distribution, and new releases keep climbing. Valve hasn't disclosed user counts since 2021.
	- Related: Steam is one of the peers in the revenue table of [[Roblox-RBLX-thesis]].
- ## Gross Revenue
	- | Period | Gross game sales | Source |
	  | --- | --- | --- |
	  | 1H 2026 | **\$11.1B**, +14.5% YoY, Steam's biggest half-year ever | DemandSage (Alinea) |
	  | 2025 | **About \$20B** (Alinea); **\$16.2B through November** (Quantumrun) | Both |
	  | 2024 | \$10.8B | Both (PC Gamer series) |
	  | 2023 | \$8.7B | Both |
	  | 2022 | \$7.6B (DemandSage) vs. \$12.0B (Quantumrun) | Conflicting |
	  | 2021 / 2020 | \$7.4B / \$7.2B | DemandSage |
	  | 2019 / 2018 | \$4.5B / \$5.3B | DemandSage |
	- **1H 2026 vs. 2H 2025**: 1H 2026 was 8% higher than 2H 2025, even though the second half usually wins because of the winter sales.
	- *My math*: \$11.1B / 1.145 implies 1H 2025 of about \$9.7B, and \$11.1B / 1.08 implies 2H 2025 of about \$10.3B. Together that's about \$20.0B, which matches Alinea's 2025 figure.
	- **Series mismatch**: going from \$10.8B in 2024 to about \$20B in 2025 would be +85%. The 2024 figure comes from a different series (PC Gamer) than Alinea's 2025 estimate, so the two years aren't directly comparable.
	- **Alinea's long-run series**: about \$5.5B in 2017 to about \$20B in 2025.
	- **Valve's take** (Quantumrun): a standard 30% commission brought Valve **over \$4B** in direct earnings in 2025. Game sales were 61% of platform revenue, and DLC plus microtransactions 27%.
- ## Users and Engagement
	- **Valve's last official disclosure (2021)**: **132M MAU and 69M DAU**, with 52% of MAU active daily. DemandSage notes no newer official figures exist.
		- | Year | MAU | DAU |
		  | --- | --- | --- |
		  | 2017 | 67M | 33M |
		  | 2018 | 90M | 47M |
		  | 2019 | 95M | — |
		  | 2020 | 120M | 62.6M |
		  | 2021 | 132M | 69M |
		- **Conflict**: Quantumrun reports 132M MAU / 62.6M DAU for "early 2024" and 147M / 69M for Q1 2025. Those 2024 figures match Valve's 2020–21 disclosures, so its series is probably mislabeled.
	- **Peak concurrent users (SteamDB)**: this is the timely engagement metric.
		- | Month | Peak concurrent users |
		  | --- | --- |
		  | Jan 2025 | 39.34M |
		  | Mar 2025 | 41.24M |
		  | Oct 2025 | 41.67M (record at the time) |
		  | Jan 2026 | 42.04M (record) |
		  | **Mar 29, 2026** | **42.69M (all-time record)** |
		  | Jul 2026 | 41.32M |
		- Peak concurrent users have been flat at about 41–43M since October 2025. Quantumrun puts the 2025 record at +24% YoY versus 33.6M in 2024.
		- Peak players in-game reached 13.39M in January 2026.
	- **Playtime** (Quantumrun):
		- Average weekly playtime rose from 6.3 hours in 2024 to 7.1 hours in 2025.
		- Only 15% of 2024 playtime went to games released in 2024, and nearly half went to titles 1–7 years old.
		- The average user played 4 distinct games in 2024.
- ## Catalog and Releases
	- | Year | New releases (DemandSage) |
	  | --- | --- |
	  | 2015 | 2,820 |
	  | 2020 | 9,636 |
	  | 2023 | 14,031 (Quantumrun: 14,310) |
	  | 2024 | 18,478 (Quantumrun: 18,626) |
	  | 2025 | **21,344**, +15.5% |
	  | 2026 through August | 16,115 |
	- **Total catalog**: 138,226 games (DemandSage, 2026). Quantumrun counts 239,558 pieces of content including DLC, with 120,000+ playable games and 110,634 indie titles (October 2025).
	- **Long tail** (Quantumrun): about 79% of 2024 releases were "Limited Games" that hadn't reached the engagement thresholds for full community features. Only 3,964 crossed them.
	- **Generative AI** (Quantumrun, mid-2025): 7,818 titles disclosed AI use, about 7% of the catalog. One in five 2025 releases used generative AI. Valve has required AI disclosure since January 2024.
- ## Market Share and Geography
	- **Market share**: Steam has about **74–75%** of global PC digital distribution. Epic Games Store takes about 3% of third-party revenue despite 295M registered accounts (Quantumrun). DemandSage puts Steam's US share at 75%.
	- **Users by country** (Quantumrun): US 13.7M (22%), China 11.4M (19%), Russia 9.5M (16%), Brazil 4.9M (8%) and Germany 3.6M (6%). Asia-Pacific is 42% of the user base, and Steam prices in 37 currencies.
	- **Languages** (DemandSage): English 36.9%, Simplified Chinese 23.8% and Russian 10.3%.
- ## Games and Hardware
	- **Top sellers by revenue in 2025** (SteamDB): Borderlands 4, Counter-Strike 2, Helldivers 2, No, I'm not a Human, and Dying Light: The Beast.
	- **All-time peak concurrent players**:
		- PUBG: 3.26M (Jan 2018)
		- Black Myth: Wukong: 2.42M (Aug 2024)
		- Palworld: 2.10M (Jan 2024)
		- Counter-Strike 2: 1.86M (Apr 2025)
		- Monster Hunter Wilds: 1.38M (Feb 2025)
	- **Steam Deck** (Quantumrun): 5.6M+ units sold, and 330M+ hours played in 2024, up 67%. SteamOS 3.7.8 added support for third-party handhelds in May 2025.
	- **Hardware survey, July 2025** (Quantumrun): AMD CPUs passed 40% for the first time, at 40.39% vs. Intel's 59.52%. Windows 11 is on 68% of systems.
