- ## What the Figures Establish

	- **Growth is decelerating, not revenue shrinking.** The sequential slowdown raises the importance of repeat usage and durable monetization; the excerpt cannot identify whether the cause is demand normalization, capacity limits, pricing, or product mix.
	- **The distillation disclosure qualifies revenue quality and the growth slowdown.** Distillation demand is real paid usage, but it comes from competitors extracting model capabilities rather than durable end-user workflows. Anthropic's decision to suppress it trades near-term revenue for protection of its model advantage, so part of Q3's deceleration may be policy-driven rather than evidence of weaker underlying customer demand. The disclosed range is not precise enough to quantify that effect.
	- **Adjusted profitability is encouraging but incomplete.** A positive adjusted operating margin does not establish cash generation sufficient to fund future compute obligations. Applying the reported year-to-date margin to Q3 revenue would mix periods and is not justified.
	- **Capacity commitments increase the importance of sustained demand.** They can secure supply for expansion, but could create a mismatch if contracted capacity outgrows monetizable usage. The excerpt does not provide enough contractual detail to calculate annual cash requirements or assume uniform spending over the decade.
	- **IPO timing remains a reported expectation.** The excerpt anticipates an offering the following month before Thanksgiving; it does not provide a confirmed date. The proposed valuation range should therefore be treated as pre-IPO investor commentary, not established market pricing.
